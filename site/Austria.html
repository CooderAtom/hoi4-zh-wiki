 奥地利 is a minor country in Central Europe with a population of 6.43 M. It borders  德国和 捷克斯洛伐克 to the north,  瑞士 to the west,  意大利和 南斯拉夫 to the south and  匈牙利 to the east.

历史背景

The Republic of German-Austria (Or Republik Deutschösterreich or Deutsch-Österreich) was formed in 1918 after the abdication of the Emperor Carl I with the Social Democrat Karl Renner as Chancellor of Austria. And as one of the defeated powers of WWI Austria was forced to signed the Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye. The territory of Austria was greatly reduced to 25% of its original territory, forced to reduce the army to 30 000 and to pay war reparations. Ethnic Germans were separated from Austrian lands as a result. To make it worse, the newly landlocked Austria lost their ability to trade via the sea, causing their economic situation to collapse. Austria also became dependent on its neighbors for trade, particularly the newly formed Weimar Republic to the north.

In 1927, during a political clash in Schattendorf, an old man and a child were shot and killed by Heimwehr. On 14 July 1927 the shooters were acquitted and left-wing supporters began a massive protest during which the Ministry of Justice building was burned. To restore order police and army shot and killed 89 people and injured 600. The huge protest is known as the July Revolt of 1927. Social Democrats called for a general strike which lasted four days.

After the 1927 events the conservative elements became stronger and violence in Austria continued to escalate until the early 1930s when Engelbert Dollfuss became Chancellor.

In 1938 Austria was annexed by  德意志国.

国策

奥地利国策树。
主条目：Austrian national focus tree
主条目：Austro-Hungarian joint national focus tree branches

 奥地利 gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 诸神黄昏 expansion. Without the expansion, Austria utilizes the 通用国策树. Should  奥地利 complete the focuses 多瑙河联邦 or Demand Hungarian Submission or if  匈牙利 completes the focuses The Lands of the Crown of Saint Stephen or 多瑙河联邦 either as a vassal or a leader, it gives Austria a new branch.

The Austrian national focus tree can be divided into 7 branches and 14 sub-branches and 1 joint branch:

Repeal the May Constitution Branch
The branch allows Austria to repeal the May Constitution, end Austrofascism, and legalize political opposition. It strengthens 民主主义和共产主义 support while reducing fascist influence, stabilizes the country, and introduces new political advisors and operatives. It opens up to the 共产主义, 民主主义和Monarchist sub-branches.
  • Austromarxism Sub-branch
The start of this section gives Austria the Austromarxism modifier, bans the Austrian 法西斯主义 party and sets up a civil war. During the civil war, Austria may request aid from the  苏联 or raid the old stockpiles and go it alone. It may either join the Comintern or create a communist faction. Austria will then be able to expand communism in the Danube and may then go to war with the  苏联 or  德国.
  • Gain Schutzbund Support/Intervention in Spain/Universität Wien Branch
These sections are shared between the 共产主义和民主主义 sub-branches. The top section gains militia units. The middle section allows Austria to help  共和西班牙 in their civil war. the bottom section gives research bonuses.
  • Hold Elections Sub-branch
The 民主主义 sub-branch allows the country to create a republic with support from the democratic majors. It may either join the allies or create an Alpine faction.
  • The Right to Rearm Sub-branch
The shared 民主主义 and Danubian Federation sub-branch allows the removal of the Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye, gain military and industry bonuses and an Intelligence agency.
  • Deal with the German Threat Sub-branch
This shared 共产主义, 民主主义 and Danubian Federation focuses allow Austria to go to war with  德国和法西斯主义 countries.
  • The Danubian Federation Sub-branch
This path creates the Danubian Federation and brings other countries to the faction, including 民主主义 or 中立主义  德国 or the  苏联. It allows the Austro-Hungarian joint branch. It allows the creation of the  欧洲联邦 or the Danubian State of Austria.
  • Reinstate the Herrenhaus Sub-branch
The beginning of the Monarchist path. It leads to both the main Monarchist path and the Danubian Federation path. It reinstalls 奥托·冯·哈布斯堡 as leader, or 恩斯特·吕迪格·施塔海姆贝格 if  匈牙利 reinstalled 奥托·冯·哈布斯堡 as their king.
  • The Legacy of an Empire Sub-branch
This path restores the Austrian Empire. It sets up the nation with industry, 稳定度, 战争支持度, military bonuses and an intelligence agency for further expansion.
  • The War Against Bolshevism Sub-branch
This shared Danubian Federation and monarchist focuses allow Austria to go to war with the  苏联和共产主义 countries.
  • Demand Hungarian Submission Sub-branch
The subjugation sub-branch for the monarchist branch puppets  匈牙利 and other Austro-Hungarian nations which unlocks the The Austro-Hungarian joint branch. It also gives historically Austrian states from  意大利 and cores them.

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为奥地利建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

Austria is an extremely difficult nation to play. The Germans are on your border, and will attempt to annex you. You have until around 1937-1938 to prepare. Focus on the industry branch of the focus tree, getting as many military factories as possible. You should then focus on the political branch. Going democratic or communist is ideal, so you can join a major faction when the Germans invade. Remaining fascist can get you manpower though, if you decide to remain fascist, ally Italy if possible, or Japan, though allying with Japan is less rewarding. You should make a lot of weaker infantry divisions, instead of trying to get ideal stats. This tends to make the AI hesitant to attack your lines. When the Germans attack you will mainly want to hold out in the mountains. If you are not playing iron man, you should save before the Germans press for Anschluss, because if the Germans make it through the mountains, they will have a stronger attack. Once you make your defensive line, you can just wait until the German AI overwhelms itself. Joining the Allies or Comintern will speed this up. Once you defeat the Germans, your army should be big enough to establish dominance in central Europe.

The Anschluss Risk can be mitigated by finishing the focus "Reform the Central Powers", which causes Germany to be unable to do the "Anschluss" focus. It is possible to immediately dissolve the Central Powers faction upon finishing the focus and create a new faction with Italy. This will still cause Bulgaria and Turkey to join the faction, thereby creating a decently sized mediterran faction.
