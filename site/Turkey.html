 土耳其 is a regional power in Eurasia with a population of 16.27 M. Strategically located at the crossroads of Asia and Europe, Turkey is bordered by  保加利亚和 希腊 to the west,  意大利, the Island of Rhodes to the southwest, the  苏联 to the northeast, the  叙利亚共和国 to the southwest,  伊拉克 to the south,  伊朗 to the east and the  英国 in the island of Cyprus in the Mediterranean Sea south of Turkey. Turkey has a coastline on the Black Sea to the north, the Mediterranean Sea to the south, and the Aegean-Bosphorus Sea to the west.

历史背景

The  奥斯曼帝国 entered The Great War on the side of the Central Powers and was ultimately defeated. Following the Armistice of Mudros on 30 October 1918, the victorious Allied Powers sought to partition the Ottoman state through the 1920 Treaty of Sèvres. The occupation of Istanbul and Izmir by the Allies in the aftermath of World War I prompted the establishment of the Turkish National Movement, and the Turkish War of Independence was waged with the aim of revoking the terms of the Treaty of Sèvres. By 18 September 1922, the occupying armies were expelled, and the Ankara based Turkish Grand National Assembly (TGNA), which had declared itself the legitimate government of the country on 23 April 1920, started to formalize the legal transition from the old Ottoman into the new Republican political system. The republic was officially proclaimed on 29 October 1923 in Ankara, the country's new capital.

Turkey was neutral until several months before the end of the war, at which point it joined the Allies. Prior to the outbreak of war, Turkey signed a Mutual Aid Pact with  法国 and the  英国 in 1939. After the German invasion of France, however, Turkey remained neutral, relying on a clause excusing them if military action might bring conflict with the USSR, which, after the division of  波兰, Turkey feared. Then, in June of 1941, after neighboring  保加利亚 joined the Axis and allowed the  德意志国 to move troops through to invade  南斯拉夫和 希腊, Turkey signed a non-aggression pact with Germany. Turkey was an important producer of chromite, which is a key ingredient in the manufacture of stainless steel and refractory brick and Germany had limited access to it. The key issue in Turkey's negotiations with both sides was the sale of chromite to Germany or to the Allies. The Allies had access to other sources and mainly bought the chromite in order to preclude its sale to Germany. Turkey halted its sales to Germany in April, 1944 and broke off relations in August. Turkey declared war on the Axis powers in February, 1945, after the Allies made its invitation to the inaugural meeting of the United Nations (along with the invitations of several other nations) conditional on full belligerency. No Turkish troops ever saw combat.

土耳其国策树。
主条目：Turkish national focus tree

 土耳其 gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 博斯普鲁斯之战 expansion. Without the expansion, Turkey utilizes the 通用国策树.

The Turkish national focus tree can be divided into 2 branches and 1 joint branch. The second can be divided into 3 sections with multiple sub-branches within:

The Saadabad Pact Branch
This joint branch shared with  阿富汗,  伊拉克和 伊朗 builds up the The Saadabad Pact national spirit with industry an military bonuses.
The Montreux Convention Branch
This branch allows Turkey to manipulate its internal politics.

The Montreux Convention Branch can be divided into 6 sections which pivot Turkey's internal politics.

  • Privatize the Anadolu Agency Sub-branch
This sub-branch allows  土耳其 to become 民主主义, integrate the Kurdish states and increase infrastructure. If Adnan Menderes is the leader, it will force a civil war.
  • The Sanayiciler Sub-branch
The industry sub-branch allows  土耳其 to remove its Debt Council and gain foreign investments with the help of the old Great Powers and the United States. Turkey must not be 共产主义.
  • Treaty of Saadabad Sub-branch
This focus is only available if the DLC 帝国坟场 is not enabled. It forms the Saadabad Pact with  阿富汗,  埃及,  伊朗,  伊拉克 or  叙利亚.
  • Assess Our Future Sub-branch
This sub-branch allows  土耳其 to be neutral internationally, have non-aggression pacts with middle eastern nations, take the state of Hatay and either following or deny The Legacy of Atatürk.
  • Peace at Home/ Reinvigorate Turkish Nationalism Sub-branch
This 中立主义 sub-branch allows  土耳其 to deal with the Kurdish, Kemalist and Traditionalist factions. If the Kemalist Officers have too much power and Turkey has low stability, it will start a civil war.
  • Rehabilitate the Kadro Movement Sub-branch
This sub-branch allows  土耳其 to become 法西斯主义 or 共产主义. It also allows the sending of volunteers to the Spanish Civil War.

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为土耳其建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

Turkey begins in 1936 as a very powerful country compared to their neighbors. Due to the large military and medium-sized navy to their neighbors, following the Industrial branch of the default focus tree is imperative for a successful army. Construction technology is specifically important, so using your 3 research slots for the industrial technologies is a good idea. After building up your civilian factories, military factories should be constructed. Since Turkey normally does not join a faction, you have enough time to build up. Focus on Greece first since you two have unfinished business since 1922. You will get nice factories and resources from Greece, then focus on Iraq for oil, then Bulgaria and Yugoslavia if they aren't guaranteed and world tension is low, unless Germany or Italy attacked them first. You can join either the Axis, Allies or Comintern but the Axis faction is advised. You can get French and British colonies in Middle East and Saudi Arabia and Yemen you lost in World War One. Then you can finally reform the Ottoman Empire again, this will give you cores on Iraq, Libya, Tunisia, the Dodecanese, Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Egypt, Syria, Lebanon, Jordan and Palestine. You're a vengeful, powerful country now so it's all up to you how you proceed from after this.

Alternatively, you could first invade into Iran and then Afghanistan. This allows you to attack the Chinese United Front and ally with Japan. Once this war is over, create a Chinese puppet and give it only the land bordering Japan at first, then everything else that Japan does not puppet. If you have chosen fascism, then you should now have practically unlimited manpower from the puppet. If Japan starts to justify a war on your puppet, focus on that front and defeat Japan on the Asian mainland, giving all the land to your puppet to make it extremely powerful. Soon, your puppet will have built up hundreds of divisions that despite their low quality can be used to conquer the axis, assuming they are still fighting the Soviets. If you don't border any axis nations, first declare war on a nation in the axis, then on a nation you border, so they join the axis. If Romania has joined the axis then you can ally with the Soviets to gain naval supremacy in the Black Sea and then launch a naval invasion, bypassing Bulgaria. Once the axis is defeated it is up to you to decide how to split the land and who should be attacked next.

Turan guide

Establishing Turan in game will require attacking the Comintern, and along with it, either Axis, or Allies for the last part of Syrian Turcoman territory. Since the communist Turkey path guarantees insane levels of industrial buildup, it's often one of the best ways is to go "Communist Turkey", take all the heavyweight Etatist decisions (coup de grace with nationalizing everything) and rush Greece (and if possible, Romania and/or Bulgaria) for the extra land and industry to confront the USSR. Attack Greece as early as possible to draw Romania as a co-defender, eat Greece and invade Romania through the black sea. Eat all the neighboring neutrals like Iraq, Iran, Afghanistan (Turan Requirement)until Barbarossa starts but use the German "Join the Axis" focus branch for the "Increase German Military Aid" and drop out of the Axis. If you can time your Communist sub branch industrial buildup along with German Axis aid, Clodius Agreement (be sure to increase chrome mines) and Chester Concession, you should have bulked up considerably, and armed enough to surprise and swarm the Soviet lines. Just keep pushing as north and past Crimea as early possible to block the Axis advance and make the Reich focus on defending the West. Keep a good eye on who holds the French Syria because it's the final part of the Turan quest, and antagonizing the Allies (definitely with USA on board) might be lethal.

When annexing the Soviets, do your best to block German annexations, take the Turan required provinces (and many more) and focus on Misak-ı Millî focus. With all the Turanist advisors on board, integrating cores along the Caucausus might even be an afterthought since TWO have non-core manpower bonuses; it is now only a matter of time to take Syria, squashing (hopefully Vichy) France, and focus with all one's might to the Western Front, rush French Syria, declare Turan and put tens of millions of manpower (on volunteer only!) to whatever world conquest you have in mind. Keep in mind that the best Turanic advisors will draw the country into fascism, hence the "Nazbol" term from a communist beginning into a fascist Khaganate.

Adopting Mass Assault to swarm the USSR and reduce supply when marching through the low-supply Asia and China might be necessary, and since Turkey has enormous Chrome deposits, Heavy Tank Destroyers or Heavy Tanks can be a decent choice pushing beyond the Caucasus and into Ukraine.

Balkan Entente war guide

Follow the Balkan Entente line of the focus tree and be ally with Greece, Yugoslavia and Romania before Italy declares war on Greece. When joined to war, kick Italians out of Balkans, hold Bulgaria back of the war (Balkan Entente's existence is an enough threat to hold it back) or invade, support Allies to conquer Northern Africa to secure southern front. Support Yugoslavia to hold Axis in the northern front. Attack Italy with naval invasion through Adriatic Sea to Taranto and capitulate it. You can also attack to German Reich from Yugoslavia to Eastern Austria, which has better terrain to move your troops than the Western side.

Quick take over Vichy France guide

After you became fascists and were guaranteed by German Reich and joined Axis you can start 1 vs 1 war against Vichy France which Germany will not join. You can start quick war goal against Hatay as it is Turkish core. Then you will easily invade French Syria, and if you have enough air force and navy you can quickly finish off French mainland and north Africa by naval invading through Italy. With this method you can puppet many French colonies as well as Occitania so you will have access to many steel and aluminum resources.

Finishing UK early guide

You can very easily jump and naval invade Denmark over Germany and after war is over you will have Faroe Islands. With Faroe you can easily invade Ireland next and can become neighbor to the UK for future naval invasion. You can also use Greenland for quick access to British lands in Canada, North America.
