The Career profile page shows statistics, non-steam achievements and various customizations. The visibility for other can be set to visible for other players, for only friends or private.

生涯档案

This page tracks overall statistics of all games the player has played. Sections of the page will be unlocked depending on the DLC the player has enabled.

Awards

The awards page gives the player alternative, in game achievements and goals outside the steam 成就. The awards are available without ironman mode. Unlocking them allows customization of the top section of the career profile page.
