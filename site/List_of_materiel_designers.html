关于带有反抗暴政的军用物资设计商，见Material Equipment MIOs列表。

这是materiel designers列表，位于钢铁雄心 IV中。

Materiel designers are 理念 that can be bought for 政治点数 (usually 150). They provide 科研 speed increases and stat bonuses to infantry technology and equipment. Only one materiel designer can be selected at a time, but it can be removed for free or replaced at the price of the replacement. Besides political power, many materiel designers have other prerequisites that must be completed before they are able to be selected and some are only available when certain DLC is enabled.
