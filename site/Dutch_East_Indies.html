The  荷属东印度 is a  荷兰 傀儡国 in Southeast Asia with a population of 48.53 M (core) and another 14.26 M (non-core). It borders  澳大利亚 to the east,  葡萄牙 Timor to the southeast, and  英属马来亚 to the north.

历史背景

The Dutch East Indies formally came into existence in January 1st 1800 when the Dutch government seized the assets of Dutch East India Company (Vereenigde Oostindische Compagnie or the VOC) which had declared bankruptcy. After a short French and British interregnums during the Napoleonic Wars, the colony was returned to the Dutch rule and promptly expanded colonial rule in the archipelago, finally finished after the defeat of Atjeh rebels in 1912 and the conquest of Vogelkop (Bird's Head Peninsula) in 1920.

Politically, the colony experienced a small, but continuous, reform since the implementation of Ethical Policies (Ethische Politiek) in 1901, which has resulted in formation of several political parties such as Indische Partij (led by Ernest Douwes Dekker), Partai Nasional Indonesia (led by Sukarno), Partai Komunis Indonesia (led by Semaun), and Partai Indonesia Raya (led by Dokter Sutomo). But flare-ups from revolutionary elements in the 1926 Communist Rebellion has significantly pushed back the reforms through a political crackdown which has lasted until 1935. Thus, the Volksraad (People's Council) that has been formed as a quasi-parliament for a future independent Indies government has also had its power significantly reduced compared to the Governor-General's. Furthermore, along with the Indonesian communists, the leaders of the nascent Indonesian nationalist movement has also jailed in the crackdown, such as Sukarno to the island of Flores and Mohammad Hatta and Sutan Syahrir to Boven-Digoel (at Dutch New Guinea).

Economically, the colony is at a deep crisis, having its economy plummeted by De Malaise (otherwise known as the Great Depression). Wages were being cut down and even the projects related to Ethical Policies were stopped to also helped bearing the economic burden from the  荷兰' own Crisisjaren. Exports such as oil, rubber, and cash crops were also plummeted with European and American markets fizzled out because of the ongoing crisis, with a new player,  日本 has gaining more and more shares of both import and export markets from the East Indies. The Directory of Economic Affairs under the leadership of George Charles Hart and his vice-director Hubertus Johannes van Mook has mitigated De Malaise's worst effects by giving land grants to the village-oriented plantations and public ownership of communications and finance facilities as welfare measures to keep the populace calm, to the dismay of Dutch industrialists.

Reeling with the effects of the Malaise, inadequate military in both weaponry, manpower, and discipline, and also the reactionary colonial government maintaining its repressive power, the East Indies is at a crossroads in 1936.

国策

通用国策树。
主条目：Dutch East Indies national focus tree

The  荷属东印度, lacking a unique national focus tree, uses the generic national focus tree instead. It is set to receive its own unique national focus tree in the "Thunder at our Gates" Expansion Pack DLC.

通用国策树有5个主要分支：

陆军建设分支
提供陆军经验，并提供陆军科研的科研加成。
航空建设分支
提供空军经验、建造空军基地，并提供空军科研的科研加成。
海军建设分支
提供海军经验、建造海军船坞，并提供海军科研的科研加成。
工业建设分支
建造民用工厂与军用工厂（通常即使建造槽已满也可建造）、基础设施，并给予额外科研槽。
政治建设分支
让国家可以选择要走的政治路线。

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为荷属东印度建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

Overview of the Dutch East Indies situation

The Dutch East Indies is one of the most resource-rich nations in the world. With massive room for industrial growth, the country faces a few problems: it is an Integrated Puppet of the Netherlands, it has several states that haven't been cored yet, the industry is small and lacking equipment. To break free, you have to increase your Autonomy from Integrated Puppet to Free. Fortunately, all it is not lost. And it doesn't have to rely on the grind, as the Dutch East Indies has a focus tree.

Strategy for Obtaining Independence Peacefully; TAOG

If you choose this path, you will need to build at least 15 Military Factories in order to produce any equipment at hand. Along with your civilian industry by 12 factories. Last but not least 10 Naval Dockyards. It is recommended to use the focuses of selling rubber in order to obtain Military Factories and Civilian Factories via Decisions. Be warned to not be too absorbed on selling rubber constantly as the reducing can be noticeable. Manually, it is also best to place Military Factories in Java, Sumatra, and the Lesser Sunda Islands, as these will be the easiest islands to defend against any foe and will ensure your control over at least two of them in a Civil War. Fortunately, it won't be the case. As working on the industry part goes steady, be sure to go with the negotiations event with the Volksraad. As the status quo or the early revolution can be more of a turmoil.

The player should begin the game by taking a few focuses that makes way for the independence, gradually increasing its autonomy and as well as improving the infrastructure. And, taking decisions of the Volksraad to increase the said autonomy faster, and to increase and flip to democracy as soon as possible. And for a better leader, the player should appoint Stachouwer over de Jonge, for the increasing of democratic support. Although, both of these government-generals have their own focus branches, thus, utilizing them beforehand would give a few benefits and small gains for the nation. Once switching to Volksraad, keep Stachouwer around. And to emphasize the democratic support, appoint democratic advisers, decrease the non aligned party further until everything is taken properly. Be sure to strengthen the East Indies' industries in its each with the sums mentioned above. That and the democratic popularity at its peak, should secure and rush first on the independence focuses. Make sure that the East Indies reaches first before the Netherlands does it.

As far as research is concerned, the player should focus on researching the latest Infantry Equipment and Industry technologies in order to attend to his massive deficit of equipment and keep ahead of the Asian nations, such as Siam or Japan.

The dilemma here rests between achieving independence and the Netherlands-Indonesian Union. The first one pops open an agenda to unify the Indonesian Malayan people, including the Philippines and Ceylon, through a decision tab. The other one is by absorbing the colonial overlord and taking its non aligned leader, generals, admirals, armies, fleet, air force and even cored and non cored lands. For an early conquest kickstart, take the Netherlands-Indonesian Union. It is better to have an addition of forces than building from scratch.
