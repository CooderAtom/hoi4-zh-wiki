 波希米亚和摩拉维亚保护国 is a European fascist minor that is a puppet of Germany. When the decision, "The Reichskommissariats" is available for Germany from a series of focuses. The AI, or the player in this case, picks this decision when it has conquered the entirety of Czechoslovakia. If Czechoslovakia nation joins the Axis as a member, the decision that makes the country is unavailable for Germany. In the 1939 scenario,  波希米亚和摩拉维亚保护国 starts as a playable Reichskommissariat nation. It borders the  德意志国 to the north, west and south,  波兰 to the north east and  斯洛伐克 to the east.

The protectorate can be also formed in 我们时代的和平 via the  捷克斯洛伐克 focus Complete Capitulation.

历史背景

The military occupation of Czechoslovakia by Nazi Germany began with the German annexation of the Sudetenland in 1938, continued with the creation of the Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia, and by the end of 1944 extended to all parts of Czechoslovakia.

Following the Anschluss of Austria in March 1938 and the Munich Agreement in September of that same year, Adolf Hitler annexed the Sudetenland from Czechoslovakia on 1 October, giving Germany control of the extensive Czechoslovak border fortifications in this area. The incorporation of the Sudetenland into Germany left the rest of Czechoslovakia with a largely indefensible northwestern border. Also a Polish-majority borderland region of Trans-Olza which was annexed by Czechoslovakia in 1919, was occupied and annexed by Poland following the two-decade long territorial dispute. Finally the First Vienna Award gave to Hungary the southern territories of Slovakia and Carpathian Ruthenia, mostly inhabited by Hungarians.

After the Munich Agreement of September 1938, the Third Reich had annexed the German-majority Sudetenland to Germany from Czechoslovakia in October 1938. Following the establishment of the independent Slovak Republic on 14 March 1939, and the German occupation of the Czech rump state the next day, German leader Adolf Hitler established the protectorate on 16 March 1939, issuing a proclamation from Prague Castle.The creation of the protectorate violated the Munich Agreement.

The protectorate remained nominally autonomous and had a dual system of government, with German law applying to ethnic Germans while other residents had the legal status of Protectorate subjects and were governed by a puppet Czech administration. During World War II (1939–1945), the well-trained Czech workforce and developed industry were forced to make a major contribution to the German war economy. Since the Protectorate was just out of the reach of Allied bombers based in Britain, the Czech economy was able to work almost undisturbed until the end of the war. The Protectorate administration became deeply involved in the Holocaust in Bohemia and Moravia.

The state's existence came to an end with the surrender of Germany to the Allies in May 1945. After the war, some Protectorate officials were charged with collaborationism, but according to the prevailing belief in Czech society, the Protectorate was not entirely rejected as a collaborationist entity.

国策

通用国策树。
主条目：通用国策树

波希米亚和摩拉维亚保护国 缺少专属国策树，因此改用通用国策树。

通用国策树有5个主要分支：

陆军建设分支
提供陆军经验，并提供陆军科研的科研加成。
航空建设分支
提供空军经验、建造空军基地，并提供空军科研的科研加成。
海军建设分支
提供海军经验、建造海军船坞，并提供海军科研的科研加成。
工业建设分支
建造民用工厂与军用工厂（通常即使建造槽已满也可建造）、基础设施，并给予额外科研槽。
政治建设分支
让国家可以选择要走的政治路线。

Overview of the Protektorat Böhmen-Mähren

The Protektorat Böhmen-Mähren, despite being an in-game "nation", by Ironman choices it is almost playable, so the player only benefits playing the nation is in the 1939 scenario, with its pros and cons as a protektorat. There's another way is to release them as a puppet, only in the default scenario when as Germany has researched the reichskommissariats focuses. But, it is unplayable that way. Playing this country is a challenge all about maintaining peace and order on the owned lands of the Czechs.

波希米亚和摩拉维亚保护国

The "colony" nation can be released by historical Germany. As it is released with the available and inherited perks as possible, the national spirits that nearly cripple the chances, the instantly formed agency and a leader with two debuffs and three buffs, maintaining order and keeping the colony slightly stable. Fortunately, as stated earlier, the colony nation inherits most of Germany's technology, ranging from Tanks, planes, navy, electronics, industry and doctrines. Thus, opening opportunities to start and produce if needed necessary. However, being a colony state it has many draw backs. It has a decent amount of factories but some go to the colony's overlord, and some are unusable which have to be resorted to raise compliance above 70%. Therefore, independence and high compliance mean to get the great number of factories back up. Or with other means....

Fighting the Allies in Africa

So the quickest way to spawn infantry units is to use Reichskommissariat template units as they have a 6 battalion divisions and a military police battalions. And focus nothing more but on producing guns, and building factories manually. Fortunately, you start with an infantry unit which has just a slightly better infantry than the one producing. The war in Europe will face the Protektorat having to put up with numeral campaigns done by the Germans in the West and East lands that border Germany. Try to assist and aid once their army lead the fight against the enemy nations, and try to outmanoeuvre their troops for a quick encirclement as the enemy troops attack in a thin line. This helps with the Polish Front up to the Baltics. Whereas for the western front push them around the maginot, taking down Belgium and the Netherlands and Luxembourg, as long as your forces follow and assist the German troops. Keep in mind, active participation and gains against enemy forces will certainly contribute to an independent Protektorat Böhmen-Mähren.

Contribution Score for the Axis

The Protektorat in order to get a faster and worthy won independence has has to follow and contribute to the capitulation of the Allies. If it has taken care of the situation in the European Campaign, The Protektorat has the liberty to move their expedition to the Easter Front, if the Germans declare war on the Soviets. Wait long enough, and USA will eventually join the Allies, which it will make it harder for the Axis.

African Operation Sealion

This requires Germany(or any Axis member) to have capitulated France had has a foothold on the French soil. Produce Transport planes, and train paratroopers, and prepare to drop from Normandy on certain tiles that are undefended, favourable in East Anglia. Otherwise, train marines and maintain naval supremacy on the English Channel, and naval invade the East British coasts. Rush down to occupy every state in the British islands before Britain reinforces their defences and makes the invasion plans futile. If possible, the Germans will come in need to support the invasion once your troops have already landed. Once the Allies have capitulated, seize a few lands, some navies from minor Allies and reparations. Be sure to pay attention to the Protektorat's coring decision. As it will serve better to have two more cores than none. And unlike AOI or other Reichskommissariats, the Protektorat can legitimize itself in that aspect.

Participation in War with USSR

After the Allies have been taken care of, Germany will declare war on USSR. This requires to take the entirety of your available troops and set them in the frontline with the USSR. Make additional gains, push further into their lands with outmanoeuvres, until the crucial cities have been taken. If contributed more than enough, The Protektorat will gain independence by the player' decision.
