主条目：Welsh decisions

With the 效忠审判 DLC enabled, 威尔士亲王国 can get cores on  威尔士 via a decision.

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为威尔士亲王国建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

The Argentinian Dragon situation

威尔士阿根廷 is one of the few releasable nations, that enjoy the right of having an achievement. It has more lands compared to Wales, but not in maximum population or number of resources. Nonetheless, it has a chance for an expansion like other releasable nations  埃及,  库尔德斯坦和 萨普米. But given some smart moves and cheese you should be able to form a pretty formidable 威尔士阿根廷, given some time, effort, and luck.

The Dragon Rises fascist route

Now, the first thing you should do is take the first Political focus, which will give you 120 political power, plus the 70 you have already accumulated. Use this to get a fascist advisor. Yes, I know, going fascist is the go-to strategy for basically every minor, but if it works, it works. While you wait for fascist support to tick up, take some industrial focuses to get some free factories. Grab the two free civilian factories first, then get the three military factories. By this time you should be above fifty percent fascist support, so hold a referendum to become Ymerodraeth Patagonia. Use your excess political power to get war economy, and if you have any extra left, get some Improved Worker Conditions or Anti-Communist/Democratic raids. This will improve your stability.

At this point, you should've flipped fascist already, so start joining the Axis while Germany deals with the European theatre. At the same time start working on the political focuses. Make a template of paratrooper units, and spam it out. If your units are lacking weapons and the factories cant produce enough in time, use the international markets to make weapon purchases. You should also have a fair amount of political power at this point, so take some stability-increasing decisions and maybe a Captain of Industry. At the same time make an amount of research on planes, notably fighters, medium bombers, and produce at least 50 transport planes. Take the paratrooper units along with the airforce and place them in Western France, if Germany has capitulated France. Establish dropping points into UK, and join once the airforce is ready and trained. It will be time delaying once as you join the war aerial dominance has to be on your favour in order to make landings in Wales, Northern England and London. Germany in this case or any of the Axis powers will automatically provide support for the invasion. Once your paratroopers land in Wales, be sure to rush down and occupy almost all of it before the British troops defeat you in Cardiff.

The Dragon Rises by Brazilian assistance

Another way is to start off the conquest is Brazil, and you have had to annex the allies and control Great Britain especially, if you have earned the "Bad Ending" achievement. Initially, you will build up your Welsh Argentinian cores, notably military factories and dockyards. It should be easy enough, as at this point Welsh Argentina are already your core states. After that, release Wales as an independent nation. And to take some safety measures you can also delete some of the factories on Welsh cores in order to have less resistance in the process. Release Welsh Argentina, switch to play as them and uncheck "release as puppet" not to start off in the wrong foot. Welsh Argentina will possess a few technologies that you as Brazil has researched in the rest of the in game time. Build cheap submarines, only a few suffice to make amends onto naval invasion. Get military and naval access from countries near Wales, even with the political ideology path around them if the neighbouring country is fascist then go fascist. Otherwise if communist, then go communist. Otherwise, if this was done after conquering the world as Brazil, go with the ideology that Brazil has as they will release countries all over the world. As mentioned with Wales neighbours, if Brazil is communist and has a faction, join them if you flipped to communist. The same idea goes if Brazil is fascist instead, then turn fascist as well. Justify on Wales and prepare the navy for its naval invasion. And, subsequently declare war on them. Depending on the player's decision, it can either puppet or annex Wales after its capitulation.

The Dragon Rises by British assistance

Great Britain is another way to go start off the conquest too. But, in this particular situation, it cant release Wales manually. However, with the "London Naval Treaty Signatory" spirit it can bully and harass Argentina in order to get a war declaration through naval treaty decision. Depending on the situation, Argentina is prone to the British pressure and accept navy disarmament. But if it refuses, then you can declare war on Argentina and naval invade from Falkland Islands. Utilize the navy, and bring the airforce and a number of troops to commit to the invasion. Argentina will put up little resistance, but will nonetheless capitulate. In the process, release Welsh Argentina and decide the fate of the rest of the Argentinian lands. Alternatively, Argentina gets an invitation to join the LNT. If refused, this gives a war goal to all signatories. And, stick with the naval invasion program mentioned earlier. Otherwise, should the Argentinian navy disarmament occur instead, then there is another alternative. Go for the "A Change in Course" Focus, turn fascist and manually justify on Argentina. And, follow up with the fascist decision and focuses in the country in order to make manual releases easy. In the meantime, build up military, civilian factories and dockyards in the Welsh Argentinian cores while waiting on the fascist decisions to finish.

After that release Wales, and then "Play as" release Argentinian Wales. As released nation, Welsh Argentina will possess a few technologies that you as Great Britain has researched in the rest of the in game time. Again, build cheap submarines, only a few suffice to make amends onto naval invasion, after going with the Naval Effort focus. Then, with decent political power hire a chief of army for an early army experience boost. After that, go for the political effort. Go communist, and ignite a civil war. Make a template of one cavalry unit, and spam it out, and capture its capital. At the same time, justify on Republican Spain, if they are currently losing in the Spanish Civil War. Manpower can be an issue so take the "Ideological Loyalty" as the Welsh Argentinian manpower will go absent way quick. You should also have a fair amount of political power at this point, so take some stability-increasing decisions and maybe a Captain of Industry. And at the same time, justify on Wales. Once your war justification on Republican Spain is completed, declare war on them, and ask military access on Nationalist Spain. Nationalist Spain will be your stepping stone for the naval invasion of Wales. With the justification finished, declare war on Wales and initiate the invasion. Wales wont have any troops deployed at this point thus it would make it easy for the invading troops to land in. Like in the previous strategy and depending on the player's decision, it can either puppet or annex Wales after its capitulation.

In any scenario given, by the latest patch, Welsh Argentina can take the decision to core Wales.
