The  阿富汗王国 is a landlocked minor country in Central Asia with a population of 6.61 M. It is bordered by  英属印度 in the south and east;  伊朗 in the west; the  苏联 in the north; and  新疆 in the far northeast. The country's strategic location along the Silk Road connected it to the cultures of the Middle East and other parts of Asia.

历史背景

The political history of the modern state of Afghanistan began with the Hotak and Durrani dynasties in the 18th century. In the late 19th century, Afghanistan became a buffer state in the "Great Game" between British India and the Russian Empire.

Following the 1842 defeat of the British-Indian forces and victory of the Afghans, the British established diplomatic relations with the Afghan government and withdrew all forces from the country. They returned during the Second Anglo-Afghan War in the late 1870s for about two years to assist Abdur Rahman Khan defeat Ayub Khan. The United Kingdom began to exercise a great deal of influence after this and even controlled the state's foreign policy.

After the Third Anglo-Afghan War and the signing of the Treaty of Rawalpindi in 1919, King Amanullah Khan declared Afghanistan a sovereign and fully independent state. He moved to end his country's traditional isolation by establishing diplomatic relations with the international community and, following a 1927–1928 tour of Europe and Turkey, introduced several reforms intended to modernize his nation

The reforms quickly alienated many tribal and religious leaders. Faced with overwhelming armed opposition, Amanullah Khan was forced to abdicate in January 1929 after Kabul fell to rebel forces led by Habibullah Kalakani. Prince Mohammed Nadir Shah, Amanullah's cousin, in turn defeated and killed Kalakani in November 1929, and was declared King Nadir Shah. He abandoned the reforms of Amanullah Khan in favor of a more gradual approach to modernisation but was assassinated in 1933 by Abdul Khaliq, a Hazara school student.Mohammed Zahir Shah, Nadir Shah's 19-year-old son, succeeded to the throne and reigned from 1933 to 1973.

Afghanistan maintained its neutrality in World War II.

阿富汗国策树。
主条目：Afghan national focus tree

 阿富汗 gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 帝国坟场 expansion. Without the expansion, Afghanistan utilizes the 通用国策树.

The Afghan national focus tree can be divided into 5 branches and 11 sub-branches and 1 joint branch:

Expand the Telegraph Network Branch
This industrial branch focuses on bonuses to the economy and improving the Subsistence Economy national spirit. It includes research slots, factories and railroads, but will force the player to answer to the demands of the graduates who need jobs as well as the rural folk. It also allows selling oil to foreign powers, or gaining aid from the  苏联. Continuing with the latter will unlock the 社会主义政变 Sub-branch.
  • Socialist Coup Sub-branch
This 共产主义 sub-branch installs a pro- 苏联 government and grants bonuses to army, propaganda and industry courtesy of Moscow. Then, it allows the player to gain Central Asian territories from the  苏联 and core it. It also grants expansion opportunities into  伊朗.
  • International Brigades Sub-branch
This shared focus between the 共产主义和民主主义 paths give weekly 人力
Support King Zahir Branch
This 中立主义 branch focuses on keeping the King without a 共产主义 or 法西斯主义 civil war, and opens the two consequent sub-branches.
  • Expand the King's Power-base Sub-branch
This sub-branch rids King Zahir Shah of his uncles' interference, and allows a closer cooperation with the West, modernizing and getting bonuses to economy, research and army, as well as allowing the establishment of a Republic, including potentially  Pashtunistan. It also grants a war goal on  新疆.
  • Visit Foreign Capitals Sub-branch
This shared sub-branch between the historical and democratic branches give increased 意见 with foreign majors, 可招募人口 and a division.
  • Biding Our Time Sub-branch
This historical sub-branch creates a balancing act between the  同盟国 and the  轴心国, including the ability to expel nationals of either. It also builds a dam and allows some modernization.
Against Kabul Branch
This branch explores alternative monarchist paths.
  • Contact Amanullah Loyalists Sub-branch
This alternative 法西斯主义 branch brings back the reformist yet vengeful Amanullah Khan as leader, allowing modernization and bonuses to the army, economy and resources. Then, the branch becomes very aggressive, allowing expansion into  印度, the  苏联,  伊朗,  伊拉克,  巴勒斯坦和 沙特阿拉伯. The ultimate goal here is to become the savior of Islam and to achieve the Pashtunistan Ascendancy.
  • Request German Support/Contact Rural Maliks Sub-branch
These focuses request help from  德国, or rural leaders in the event of a civil war.
  • Remember the Khost Rebellion Sub-branch
Instead of supporting Amanullah Khan as the leader of Afghanistan, the player may decide on alternative leaders. It also creates a faction with other middle eastern nations and calls  日本 for aid.
  • Crown the Golden Emir Sub-branch
This Crowns Faqir of Ipi as the Emir of Afghanistan. It supports rebels in central Asia, demands  India's North western states, and forms the Emirate of Pashtunistan. It also gives War Goals for Islamic states.
  • Crown the Bukharan Emir/Invite the Mughal Prince Sub-branch
This either crowns Sayyid Mir Muhammad Alim Khan as the Emir of Afghanistan, or Mizra Khurshid Jah Bahadur as Mughal Emir. The Player can either expand into the  苏联 as the Second Afghan Empire or reform the Timurid Empire and reclaim its borders.

This military branch focuses on bonuses to the army and improving the Quami or Nufus national spirits. 策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为阿富汗建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

The best strategy for  阿富汗 is to invade  伊朗 and later  伊拉克 and the rest of the independent Middle East while going fascist for the manpower preparing to fight the  苏联, as Afghanistan you need to use massed infantry with your very powerful infantry genius in your high command. Also, as Afghanistan, you are the only country in the region (other than the Soviets) with tanks, use this to your advantage by space marining your infantry divisions to give the armor buffs to your infantry. Afghanistan also has the largest military in the area as a minor, allowing easy conquest of Iran and Iraq. Do not attack  新疆 as that tiny mountain pass is almost impossible to break through. As Afghanistan you can align yourself with either the Allies who may help you against the Soviets, sending troops and/or equipment via the British Raj, or you can align with the Soviets and just conquer the middle east and then support the Soviets when the Germans invade, or you could do the most challenging option and join the axis and fight the Allies and the Comintern at the same time, if  日本 has conquered all of China however, when fascist you should join their faction as they can easily send troops and/or equipment to you through the previously mentioned tiny mountain pass with Sinkiang.
