 暹罗 is a minor country in Southeast Asia with a population of 14.48 M. It borders  英属缅甸 to the west,  法国 to the east, and  英属马来亚 to the south. Siam is not landlocked, with a large border on the Gulf of Thailand, and additional sea access into the Andaman Sea. It is hilly, with dense forest throughout the North of the country.

历史背景

Prior to 1932,  暹罗 was ruled by an absolute monarch. However, in 1932, a coup d'etat by the Khana Ratsadon, or People's Party, forced the monarch to abide by a constitution and yield much of his power to a civilian government. The reigning monarch, Prajadhipok or formally Rama VII, was initially accepting of a constitutional government. A 1933 counter-coup of which he played a minor role in failed after Phraya Phahon, one of the Khana Ratsadon's leading military officers, regained control over government. An armed revolt led by Prince Boworadej attempted once again in late 1933 to restore Rama VII's power, but this too failed. Continual disagreements with the Khana Ratsadon-led government under Phraya Phahon resulted in Rama VII voluntarily abdicating and go into exile in the  英国 in 1935, eventually dying there in 1941, still in exile. His son, Ananda Mahidol, who was with him in exile, was next in line for the Siamese throne.


The state of Siamese politics hung in a fragile balance, the military and civilian wings of the Khana Ratsadon frequently coming to disagreements. All it would take is one minor scandal to topple Phraya Phahon and his government from power, and this is the state that the nation is in at the game's start date in 1936.

 暹罗 is an almost an isolated country, with all diplomacy values revolving around Different Ideology, Same Ideology和Same Ruling Party. The only exception are nations that are not Non-Aligned, that start with a -10 opinion because of different ideology. But, it can be deliberately targeted by these nations:

 日本 has a numerous focuses that concerns Siam. It may ask Siam to join its faction via the Occupy Siam national focus. This may make Siam 法西斯主义. Otherwise, it can declare war via the Pressure Siam. The other focuses are Occupy Indochina, The Indochinese Soviet, Spread the Revolution South, even in its democratic expansionist paths.

 美国 may influence Siam via its "Secure Asia" focus. This can result in Siam being puppeted or invaded.

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为暹罗建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

Overview of the Siamese situation

In a starting scenario Siam is not a very powerful country. An air force on deficit, small navy and force that cant stand against on its own against the powerful neighbours, unless if it mobilizes. On historical note, it would be preferable to become Fascist, albeit joining Germany's faction or being patient enough joining up Japan. Becoming Communist is another alternative, but rather uncommon, if you are looking to join the Soviets or help Communist China withstand the Japanese invasion. Alternatively, becoming Democratic or maintaining the Non Aligned government doesn't hold up for an active war campaign, to which it will last a peaceful gameplay. With and without the DLC being enabled.

Depending on how the events play out, the main priority is to occupy the rest of Indochina, and then it is depending on the player if it wishes to proceeds for additional conquest.

Forming the Rattanakosin Empire 1939 scenario; TAOG Disabled

This formable requires Laos and Cambodia. Not a very powerful formable nation, but better than nothing. It opens up more space to manpower, and industry wise. Although, Cambodia of the two has the upper hand for additional building slots, as Laos is usually thin. And, in a 1939 scenario, Siam starts slightly with more military notably in the navy and air force section, and a tiny improvement on the infantry. Technology wise, Siam finally has done a decent research but has not implemented them into its military. And, has an absolute fascist popularity instead of non-aligned, and with a mass amount of political power. Siam can take various choices regarding political power. In 1939, with a war in China, and a brewing conflict in Europe, Siam can choose any way it can to take back its lands from France, either by Japanese assistance or by German eagerness. Or.... In its own way.

Joining the Japanese

This happens when Japan goes historical, and is primarily at war with the Chinese faction. If France refuses to hand over Indochina to the Japanese, then it will declare war. You can join them to help out Japan with it. Finally, it is possible to request control of occupied territories from Japan with sufficient war participation.

Joining the Germans

If you have become fascist, immediately join the Germany. War with France will be almost inevitable, so make sure you state your troops in the border to grab and take the lands fast before they go to Vichy France. Depending on the controller, the player can enter the war before joining Japan to occupy Indochina from France or Free France, or convince Germany to guarantee your independence and allow you to occupy Vichy territory without going to war with the Axis.

Fighting against Vichy France

This happens when Siam chooses to stay neutral a bit longer, by rejecting both Japan and Germany's requests to join each one's faction. War in Europe will finally take its toll and a few nations might fall upon the German invasions. Among them is obviously France. France has capitulated, and Vichy France will obviously take its place. Fortunately, Vichy France hasn't joined the Axis yet, and their control in Indochina remains free to get, by either Japan or Siam.

Nonetheless, immediately spam train infantry units as much as you can to match up with the other nations' army strength. And, rush down on the Industrial focus, to get as much industry and obtain the research slot possible, before turning to the political focus. Siam, has also sufficient space to built up extra industries manually, and make sure you are not in a border war threatening position to do all this. Fortunately, having a large amount of rubber and many nations trading with you, will grant you the benefit to build up quicker.

As historically occurred, Vichy France and Siam will engage in war, but this time, Siam will be the one declaring war. Seize and occupy Laos and Cambodia's victory points, and immediately form "Rattanakosin Empire" until Vichy France finds its time to reinforce their Indochinese front. Should Siam successfully occupy Vietnam in the process, it will be pressured between Japan to hand over the Indochina domains to them, or if Vichy France finally joins the Axis, both will threaten Siam with a war. At this point, Siam should opt to join the Allies, the Comintern reluctantly to resist the Axis or Japanese invasions. Or finally join the Japanese, should they accept the invitation.

Asia Colonization Status; TAOG Disabled

In this case scenario, it becomes short and easy in objectives. Laos and Cambodia are already independent with no army whatsoever, and all that you need to do is to rush down to either Fascism or Communism.

Now, the first thing you should do is take the first Political focus, which will give you 120 political power, plus the 70 you have already accumulated. Use this to get a fascist advisor. Yes, I know, going fascist is the go-to strategy for basically every minor, but if it works, it works. While you wait for fascist support to tick up, take some industrial focuses to get some free factories. Grab the two free civilian factories first, then get the three military factories. By this time you should be above fifty percent fascist support, so hold a referendum to become Siamese Empire. Use your excess political power to get war economy, and if you have any extra left, get some Improved Worker Conditions or Anti-Communist/Democratic raids. You can join Japan to assist with their conquests in China. Otherwise, if your manpower is still an issue you can go communist to get to the weekly manpower army experience.

By now you should have a total of 10 or 11 factories- 7 civilian and 4 military. One of your civilian factories will be used for consumer goods, so use the remaining one to either build some more civs or trade for some steel. Use one of your 3 military factories to produce artillery, and use the other 2 to build guns. Set some factories to build support equipment as well once you get some more mils.

At this point, you should've flipped fascist already, Siam should have a war goal on Laos and Cambodia. Declare war on them, and subsequently annex them. Finally, you can form the "Rattanakosin Empire". At the same time start working on the political focuses. Make a template of one cavalry unit, and spam it out. You should also have a fair amount of political power at this point, so take some stability-increasing decisions. Should you declare on Vietnam, Burma, Malaysia, or god forbid, India, then win against them each other can give you enough expansion and access to some valuable resources and sea access. Otherwise, declare war on Yunnan or Guanxi Clique and conquer them for expansion.

Road to unify Indochina

With the TAOG enabled, Siam gets to have its focus tree. With two national spirits that, for some cases, have to be balanced in order to avoid a civil war. The Military and Civilian Wing of the Khana Ratsadon. With each focus that the player should tread is to balance these said spirits, and take the benefits focuses when it only appeases one. And make sure to start hiring political advisors as it would help greatly to reduce these odds. For Siam and a few certain East Asian nations, manpower is abundant and the factories redundant. To counter this issue, make way for the industrial side to produce more mills and civs to get a better kickstart for the Siamese army to unify Indochina. As well as spam the Infantry template to build up a good army. Research engineering and adjust artillery support to make the infantry template a little more than decent.

It comes to attention that manually declaring war to Burma, Malaya or France via Indochina, has been not necessarily to Siam's advantage, nor joining the Axis in their time of peace. Fortunately for Siam these hard and grinding wars have been reduced to focuses on retaking said lands from those nations. Therefore Limited Wars. Take each focus respectively. The main big target has been France, considering it has most of the Indochinese lands under its possession. Ideally, for France to be dealt with, is to either be a Vichy state in order to take the lands back via war declared decisions. Capture all of the states before the time limit in the decision, white peace with France, and the lands will be acquired to Siam's. Next stop is with either Burma or Malaysia. Depending which of these said nations Siam hits, all of them will be called to invade Siam. All that it has to be done is to take the aimed provinces of the nation that Siam has decision declared war on. One with Burma, one with Malaysia and one with UK for the Perak state. White peace with them in each different scenario and focus on integrating the acquired lands.

The other looming threat is Japan, that seeks to control Indochina, or coerce Siam to join with their faction. Do not give them any land, but simply join their faction. For the player, if it prefers to join Japan in the Chinese conquest, it may want to join. Unless Japan has declared war on the Philippines or Dutch East Indies deliberately declaring war on the Allies and the US, it would pose to leave the faction. But for more skilled players, it would be suited on picking one front at the time. With an extra challenge on clearing any remnants of Malaysia or Burma or taking the fight to South Asia. And utilizing the navy.
