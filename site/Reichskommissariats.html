This page details the countries created as Reichskommissariats; for the subject type, see 法西斯主义附庸国

帝国专员辖区 are special, releasable puppet states which can only be formed by the  德意志国 in controlled enemy territory, following the completion of the focus Uplift the Rosenberg Office. They are created using decisions after conquering the relevant territory. Additional puppet states become available after completing the focuses Mittelafrika, Create Asian Reichskommisariats和Establish Protectorates in America. All of the puppet states are 帝国专员辖区, with the exception of  波希米亚和摩拉维亚保护国, which is a 帝国保护国, but for the purposes of this article will sometimes be called a Reichskommissariat. The Reichskommissariats do not have core states, but are nonetheless designed to limit the amount of  德国 occupied territory in order to reduce the negative effects of the Economy of Conquest national spirit. Almost all territory in the game can be made into Reichskommissariats, notable exceptions being  德国,  奥地利,  意大利,  法国,  瑞士,  丹麦,  瑞典,  芬兰,  马达加斯加, Crete, the Bosporus Strait, and the Suez Canal. The  波希米亚和摩拉维亚保护国 starts as an playable country in 1939, but will normally be created by  德国 if it annexes  捷克斯洛伐克 via an event during historical playthroughs.

国策

通用国策树。
主条目：通用国策树

All Reichskommissariats use the generic national focus tree.

国家精神

All 帝国专员辖区, with the exception of the  波希米亚和摩拉维亚保护国 and the  Generalgouvernement, start with the national spirit Reichskommissariat Zivilverwaltung, the effects of which depend on whether the Reichskommissar is a German or a local collaborator.

科技

All Reichskommissariats start with three research slots and inherit all technologies from  德国.

政治

All Reichskommissariats begin as 法西斯主义 puppet states of  德国 with a 法西斯主义 popularity of 60%. They get advisors and military staff of local origin. All territory under their control are occupied territory, they do not have any cores.

军事

All Reichskommissariats start with no starting army, navy, or air force. They inherit all division templates from  德国. Additionally, they start with two division templates: Militärpolizei and Berittene Militärpolizei (6 Infantry/Cavalry battalions with a Military Police company) in order to combat resistance in their territories.
