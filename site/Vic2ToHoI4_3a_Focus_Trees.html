All countries, like in Vanilla, would start with Generic National Focus. But a special focus tree is created for each of the great powers of Vic2.

Generic Focus Tree

通用国策树有5个主要分支：

  • Army Effort gives army experience and research bonuses to army research.
  • Aviation Effort gives air experience, builds air bases, and research bonuses to air research.
  • 海军投入 gives navy experience, builds naval dockyards, and research bonuses to navy research.
  • 工业努力 builds civilian and military factories (usually even if building slots are already full), infrastructure and grants extra research slots.
  • 政治努力 gives the country choices on which political path they will take.

With the exception of 政治努力, these branches are identical to Vanilla generic focus trees

There are 2 additional branches dynamically generated by the Converter in the Generating reconquest wars section:

  • Reclaim Cores starts wars to take core states, gives some political power and ideas to help prepare for war
  • Territorial Disputes adds claims on neighbors, starts wars to take claimed states and gives some war support and manpower

They are added to all the countries that don't get their special focus tree (either because they are not a Great Power or player country, or because no targets could be acquired for their generation), provided there are potential target countries.

Victoria 2 allies, subjects and overlords are ineligible to become target countries for these branches. Also, Reclaim Cores target countries are ineligible as targets for Territorial Disputes.

The number of sub-branches varies. A sub-branch will be generated for each target identified for Reclaim Cores branch. If the number of target countries for Reclaim Cores is greater than or equal to 4, then only 1 Territorial Disputes branch will be generated. Otherwise, the combined number of these branches will amount to 5.

政治努力

Depending on the selected ideologies conversion option, the Converter can add or remove branches compared to Vanilla generic focuses. The 2 new branches that can be created for converter ideologies are:

  • Absolutism focus
  • Radicalism focus

Reclaim Cores

Reclaim Cores (aka Reconquest Branch) is designed to give the country war focuses for retaking core states not owned by it. After the Reclaim Cores focus is selected, non-Fascist countries will get a modifier decreasing 稳定度 and increasing the popularity of 法西斯主义 ideology, until all the sub-branches have been completed, representing the Victoria 2 mechanism that drives countries with high revanchism towards 法西斯主义, while making them more unstable through potential rebellions. As the progress down the branch continues, the ruling party popularity will increase, depending on the number of core provinces that would potentially be reclaimed.

The impact of number of core provinces to reclaim is represented in the table below by X和Y variables, which are calculated according to these formulas:

X=Y_1+Y_2+·s +Y_i
i - total number of target countries (i.e. sub-branches)

: Y=min(P_1,10)+min(P_2,10)+·s +min(P_n,10)
P - total number of HoI4 provinces in a core state
n - number of core states the target country owns

示例
 阿根廷 has sub-branches to reclaim cores on:
  •  巴拉圭 - owns 2 Argentine core provinces in a single state: : Y_Paraguay=2
  •  玻利维亚 - owns 14 Argentine core provinces in a single state: : Y_Bolivia=10
  •  智利 - owns 9 Argentine core provinces in one state and 12 in another: : Y_Chile=9+10=19

: X=Y_Paraguay+Y_Bolivia+Y_Chile=2+10+19=31

Territorial Disputes branch war targets are determined based on Victoria 2 AI conquer province strategies (player country included). Province strategy values are summed up by owner countries to get the total value for potential targets, after which countries with value less than 10% of the top target value are discarded.

Special Focus Trees

During the "Generating major wars" section of the conversion process, Great Powers and player countries get their special focus trees, depending on their government 意识形态. At the moment, non-aligned countries never receive special trees, they are left with the generic one.

In the process, the Converter tries to find appropriate targets for the country to attack or to contain. If no targets could be identified, the special branch will not be generated and the country will get only the generic focus tree. Absolutist and Radical trees are the exception, they will always be generated regardless of targets.
