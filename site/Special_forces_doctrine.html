Special forces doctrines introduced in 反抗暴政 allow nations to further specialize usage of their special forces. With 反抗暴政 enabled, 山地兵, 海军陆战队, and 伞兵 each have two unique subdoctrine choices available. With 不妥协，不投降 enabled, players have access to the 游骑兵 special forces and their two subdoctrines as well. Players must choose which special forces they wish to elevate (and which of the two playstyles available for each special forces type to lean into) using their limited amount of [[#Special Forces Branch Specialization|special forces branch specializations], which can unlock the second doctrine track.

总体学说

Grand Doctrines represent the overall doctrine of your special forces. They provide bonuses when unlocked, as well as unique milestones for each track once mastered. Only one Grand Doctrine can be enabled at a time.

总体学说

Expanded Special Forces

  • 特种部队容量倍率：+4.0%
  • Special Forces Division training time: -20.0%
特种部队能够执行普通士兵无法执行的任务。我们需要扩大训练项目，让更多士兵取得资格。

Elite Special Forces

  • Special Forces Supply Grace: +24.0 Hour(s)
  • 特种部队：
    • 组织度：+10.0
只有最优秀者中的最优秀者才能成为特种部队。通过训练的士兵将比其他任何人都更强壮、更迅捷、作战更顽强。

学说路线

Unlike for Land doctrines, Air doctrines, and 海军学说, Special Forces tracks are not split into different categories. Each of the two tracks has access to all eight of the subdoctrines (as long as the required DLCs are enabled), with the exception that you cannot pick to fill both tracks with a subdoctrine from the same type of Special Forces. For example, players cannot pick both Elevate the Mountaineers for one track and Cold Weather Specialists for the other. Just as each track shares the same options, the options available are not impacted by the choice of grand doctrine either. 精通 is used to unlock milestones within the subdoctrine, granting further bonuses. Each track gains 精通 when certain units engage in action or at a reduced rate while training. If a subdoctrine is not selected, up to 200 精通 is held in reserve.

When a track as been mastered, it unlocks the grand doctrine milestone for the track. This holds true for Special Forces tracks too; even though they aren't unique functionally, they are still distinct. The left track of the two, which is unlocked first, has a different milestone than the right track, which is unlocked second.

Subdoctrines

Subdoctrines allow special forces to further specialize in different types of combat. Subdoctrines provide some bonuses when picked and additional bonuses are unlocked when enough 精通 is accumulated in each Doctrine Track. Only one Subdoctrine can be enabled from each type of Special Forces at a time. Grand Doctrines do not affect which Subdoctrines are available.

Unlike other doctrine types, Special Forces doctrines determine both what they gain mastery from and how much experience they cost (and of what type) from the subdoctrine, not the doctrine track.

Cold Weather Specialists / Elevate the Mountaineers All Mountaineers 60 Army Experience
Marine Commandos / Expeditionary Units All Marines and Amphibious Units 60 Navy Experience
Sabotage Parties / Concentrated Drops All Paratroopers 60 Air Experience
Jaeger Traditions / Rangers Jungle Training All Rangers 60 Army Experience

研究学说

Be aware that most countries are limited to one branch specialization. Second branch specializations can be unlocked by most major nations through their focus trees or by using the Diversify Elite Forces decision made available after 1st January, 1940.

Each individual doctrine in the  山地兵 tree has a base cost of 60 陆军经验 to unlock. This cost may be modified by spirits, advisors, and special bonuses from focuses, events, or decisions, including cost modifiers to 陆军学说 as well. The first doctrine in the tree requires the  山地步兵 I technology to unlock, as well as an available special forces branch specialization.

Each individual doctrine in the  海军陆战队 tree has a base cost of 60 海军经验 to unlock. This cost may be modified by spirits, advisors, and special bonuses from focuses, events, or decisions, including cost modifiers to 海军学说 as well. The first doctrine in the tree requires the  海军陆战队 I technology to unlock, as well as an available special forces branch specialization.

Each individual doctrine in the  伞兵 tree has a base cost of 60 空军经验 to unlock. This cost may be modified by spirits, advisors, and special bonuses from focuses, events, or decisions, including cost modifiers to 空军学说 as well. The first doctrine in the tree requires the  伞兵 I technology to unlock, as well as an available special forces branch specialization.

Branching, mutually exclusive choices within each tree can be switched at any time, but all bonuses from all unlocked doctrines after the switched choice are lost after the switch and must be re-purchased with 陆军经验, 海军经验, or 空军经验, as appropriate.

No country starts with any special forces branch unlocked in either the 1936 or 1939 scenarios.

Special Forces Branch Specialization

In addition to requiring that technology for their corresponding special forces unit be researched, the opening doctrine of each tree requires expending a special forces branch specialization (SFBS). All countries start with just one SFBS, meaning only one of the three doctrine trees can be unlocked by default.

Unlike with other doctrine types, once the first doctrine in a special forces doctrine tree is purchased, that doctrine tree is forever unlocked and a special forces specialization is forever expended. Special care therefore must be taken when choosing which doctrine tree(s) to unlock with limited special forces specialization choices, as there is no undoing the decision.

A second SFBS can be obtained using the Diversify Elite Forces decision. This decision is made available to all 主要国家 after 1st January, 1940 that do not have a 国策 that would give them a second SFBS.
