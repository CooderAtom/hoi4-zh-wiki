 伊朗 is a large country in West Asia with a population of 12.60 M. It is bordered to the west by  伊拉克和 土耳其, to the north by the  苏联 and to the east by  阿富汗和 英属印度.

历史背景

During World War I, there was an invasion by the Russian Empire ( 苏联) who were supported by the  英国 as well as by their old rivals the Ottomans ( 土耳其) who were supported by the  德意志国.

After the October Revolution, the Soviet Union tried to establish a puppet government in northern Iran, similar to  蒙古和 新疆 like the "Soviet Republic of Gilan" or after WWII the "Azerbaijan People's Government" and the "Republic of Mahabad". In 1921 there was a reaction to the growing influence of the coup with the support of the UK, which eventually led to the change to the Pahlavi Dynasty.

Although Iran declared itself neutral at the beginning of the war, there was sympathy for the Axis powers, but because of its strategic importance after the beginning of the war between the Soviet Union and Germany, the Anglo-Soviet invasion of Iran occurred in 1941.

国策

伊朗国策树。
主条目：Iranian national focus tree

 伊朗 gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 帝国坟场 expansion. Without the expansion, Iran utilizes the 通用国策树.

The Iranian national focus tree can be divided into 7 branches 17 sub-branches and 1 joint branch:

Establish a National Front Branch
This begins the path to oppose the Shah and builds ideology against Monarchism.
  • United Progressive Parties Sub-branch
This path sets Iran as a 共产主义 nation through civil war. It may ally with the  苏联 or create a 共产主义 faction in the region.
  • Industrial Aid Sub-branch
The 共产主义 industry path gives research bonuses, 石油 resources as well as nuclear bonuses.
  • The New Economy Sub-branch
This section of the tree gives military bonuses to a 共产主义 Iran.
  • International Solidarity Sub-branch
This shared sub-branch between the 共产主义和民主主义 paths allow volunteers, unlocks decisions to launch uprisings in neighboring regions, force colonial powers to leave with the threat of war, and expansion opportunities in the middle east in order to build Iranian hegemony.
  • Rally Behind Mosaddegh Sub-branch
The 民主主义 path either makes Iran into a constitutional monarchy or a republic. It will then be able to gain industry bonuses, membership in the Allies and annexation of Iraq and Afghanistan. the constitutional monarchy path, by giving the clergy power, may lead to a civil war and the overthrow of the government in order to create the Islamic Republic of Iran.
  • Unite the Ultra-Nationalists Sub-branch
The 法西斯主义 path starts a civil war and reinstalls a new Shah. It may ally with the  Germans, the  日本 or go it alone in order to conquer surrounding nations in order to form  Greater Iran. It may then expand into the middle east and India as well as conquer  德国
The Fight for Iran Branch
This branch is only available if Iran has gone into a defensive war. It gives military bonuses. It will then be split into various sections, with some parts being hidden.
  • Force Them Back Sub-branch
This aggressive path gives attack bonuses to troops. Iran can then either force peace with war reparations or force a white peace, depending on the level of war participation and surrender progress.
  • Dig in and Defend Sub-branch
This aggressive path gives defense bonuses to troops and 陆上堡垒. Iran can then either surrender and become a puppet or negotiate for a white peace with resource rights, depending on the level of war participation and surrender progress.
  • Rebuilding Iran sub-branch
If Iran becomes a puppet, this branch will then be visible. It either swears loyalty to its overlord and get bonuses with it, or it can plot for an overthrow of the overlord and declare independence.
  • Reinstate the Qajar Dynasty sub-branch
If Iran becomes a puppet of the  英国 it may get this focus installing Mohammad Hassan Mizra of the Qajar Dynasty as Shah of Iran. This dynasty was the former shahs before the Pahlavi dynasty took power.
  • Retake North Iran/Consolidate British Territory Sub-branch
These focuses are available from two countries. When  英国 finishes the Secure Iran or the  苏联 finishes the Preemptive Invasion of Iran focus to start the Operation Countenance event chain. There, Iran refuses  英国/ 苏联 pressure, then the  英国/ 苏联 requests  苏联/ 英国 aid and the  苏联/ 英国 accepts. It is then required for Iran to Swear Fealty to the Invaders. The focuses then allow Iran to declare war against the  苏联 to retake the lost northern territory and core the  英国 states across the Persian Gulf.
The Pahlavi Imperium Branch
This starts the Monarchist branch with 政治点数
  • To Stand With Giants Sub-branch
The historical branch brings positive relations with  德国 for trade and research bonuses. It can also ban 共产主义 and give 军用工厂 bonuses. It may also then remove the Anglo-Iranian Oil Company as well as declare war against the  苏联 and releasing puppets in central Asia. The other side of the branch gives industry bonuses.
  • Forced Secularization Sub-branch
This shared focus secularizes Iran. It affects other focuses in other sub-branches.
  • A Legacy of Greatness Sub-branch
This sub-branch of the tree sets the Pahlavi dynasty as the center of the nation. The leftmost section assassinates Reza Shah Pahlavi and brings his son, Mohammad Reza Pahlavi as Shah. It may then create the  第三波斯帝国. The rightmost path keeps Reza Shah Pahlavi as Shah and creates the  First Iranian Empire

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为伊朗建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

As Iran the first thing you want to do is begin justifying on Iraq so you can get more resources for when you reform the Persian Empire. Once you take out Iraq you can turn your focus onto Afghanistan and take them over too. Now from here on out you will need to declare war on France and Britain so prepare your forces on the borders of their colonies and attack them. You will need Syria, Jordan, and Pakistan in order to do this. After you've preferably peaced out the European Powers you can go and invade Turkey. They shouldn't pose much of a threat to you at this point as your army should be large. Once you've taken Turkey you can take Greece if you feel like it but now you've basically reformed the Persian Empire! You are probably a great power by now so taking over the world should be easy from here.

If you don't feel lucky and want to play it safe, the best option will be to become communist and join the Soviet Union as soon as possible, since you share borders with it, enjoying its protection will guarantee your survival, if you have time, try to take Iraq for it's manpower and resources once you have joined the Comintern, it will be easier with Soviet help, still try to take Afghanistan if you still have time, make sure you help as much as possible in the soviet wars to get military experience and improve your units, at the same time focus on the navy so you can defend the Persian gulf and fortify the border with the British Raj since the UK has approaches to declare war on Iran and will most likely call the Raj to war since they share a border.
