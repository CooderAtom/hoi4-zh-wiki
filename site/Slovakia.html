 斯洛伐克 is a landlocked  德国 帝国专员辖区 in Central Europe with a population of 243 万 in 1939. It borders the  德意志国 to the northwest,  波希米亚和摩拉维亚保护国 to the west,  匈牙利 to the south, and  波兰 to the north and east. It appears as a starting nation in the 1939 start date only, being otherwise a releasable country.

历史背景

The idea of Slovakia was born during the nineteenth century by the Slovak nationalists. Today, Czech and Slovak are considered two different (but similar) languages (both are part of the West Slavic branch of the Slavic languages), despite their high level of mutual intelligibility. However, in the Interwar period, the official ideology of  捷克斯洛伐克 embraced the idea of these two ethnic groups being in fact two branches of the same nation. Despite this, Slovaks view Czechs as a different ethnicity.

Interwar  捷克斯洛伐克 had a sizeable German minority of around 3 million people living in a region bordering Germany, known as Sudetenland. After the Munich agreement in late September 1938, these areas were awarded to Germany, and subsequently, parts of Slovakia were given to Poland and in particular, to Hungary. Weakened,  捷克斯洛伐克 was ultimately split in March 1939, with Czech lands being annexed to  Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia, and Slovakia forming  (First) Slovak Republic (also known as the Slovak State) under German overlordship.

国策

带有奥匈联合国策树分支的通用国策树。
主条目：通用国策树
主条目：Austro-Hungarian joint national focus tree branches

 斯洛伐克, lacking a unique national focus tree, uses the generic national focus tree instead. However, should  奥地利 complete the focuses 多瑙河联邦 or Demand Hungarian Submission or if  匈牙利 completes the focuses The Lands of the Crown of Saint Stephen or 多瑙河联邦 as vassal, it gives Slovakia a new branch.

通用国策树有5个主要分支：

陆军建设分支
提供陆军经验，并提供陆军科研的科研加成。
航空建设分支
提供空军经验、建造空军基地，并提供空军科研的科研加成。
海军建设分支
提供海军经验、建造海军船坞，并提供海军科研的科研加成。
工业建设分支
建造民用工厂与军用工厂（通常即使建造槽已满也可建造）、基础设施，并给予额外科研槽。
政治建设分支
让国家可以选择要走的政治路线。


The Austro-Hungarian branch can be divided into 4 sections:

Reforming the Common Army Sub-branch
Gives army experience and research bonuses to army research, and as well as national spirits for every type of infantry unit.
Economic Cooperation Policy Sub-branch
Gives economic experience and research bonuses in joint cooperations, as well as national spirits for economy.
Joint Industrial Planning Sub-branch
Builds civilian factories (usually even if building slots are already full), infrastructure and grants national spirits for the industry wise.
Convene the Imperial Diet/United in the Face of Danger Sub-branch
这由匈牙利或奥地利执行，会对每个成员的国家精神做最终修正，并给予少量政治点数，代价是每日损失自治度。

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为斯洛伐克建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

If you want to free yourself from Germany before the start of the war, you'll have to start a civil war, and win it quickly, usually a week. You should win it quickly otherwise Germany will get called in. To start the civil war, hire a Communist Revolutionary, do Anti-Democratic Raids, Prepare for Civil War, then Ignite Civil War. (You can also do Democratic Reformer and Anti-Communist raids) You should hire the Army Maneuver Expert and push for the enemy capital, Presov, with both units (infantry and tank). If you manage to take their capital before Germany is called in, you will win the civil war, and you will be a free nation.
