 埃塞俄比亚 is a minor country in East Africa with a population of 9.91 M. It is bordered by colonies of the  英国 to the west and  英国 Somaliland to the northeast,  法国 Somaliland to the north, the  奥萨苏丹国 to the northeast, and  意大利 on the frontier with Eritrea in the north and Somalia to the south. It starts at war with  意大利, which seeks to annex the region into its other East African colonial holdings.

历史背景

Ethiopia was attacked by  意大利 in 1935, leading to the Second Italo-Abyssinian War (Ethiopia had previously repelled an Italian attack in 1895-1896 during the First Italo-Abyssinian War) which ended with an Italian victory and the annexation of Ethiopia into Italian East Africa. Ethiopia remained under Italian control until 1940 when the Allies launched the East African campaign resulting in an Allied victory and the Allied capture of Italian East Africa in 1941, allowing Ethiopia to effectively restore its sovereignty. Mussolini's government collapsed and Italy capitulated officially in 1943, with Ethiopia's independence being formally restored in the Paris Peace Treaty in 1947.

国策

埃塞俄比亚国策树。
主条目：Ethiopian national focus tree

 埃塞俄比亚 gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 唯有鲜血 expansion. Without the expansion, Ethiopia utilizes the 通用国策树.

The Ethiopian national focus tree can be divided into 6 branches and 15 sub-branches, 4 of which are hidden until certain actions are taken in game:

Ecole Militaire Haile Selassie 1er Branch
This branch gives buffs to Ethiopia’s army, navy, and air force. It allows the upgrading of the Traditional Warfare, Air Force in Infancy和 No Naval Tradition 国家精神. It also allows the removal of Chitet征兵法令
Develop Shewa Branch
This branch gives Ethiopia factories, railroads, dockyards, and research slots. The leftmost section modifies and removes the Corrupt Bureaucracy national spirit. The middle section builds infrastructure and allows Ethiopia to form the  Horn of Africa. The rightmost section gives construction bonuses as well as gives foreign designers.
The Second Italo-Ethiopian War Branch
This branch gives Ethiopia bonuses in certain states to strengthen them in the fight against  意大利. The focuses on the left gives Ethiopia offensive bonuses to their units while the focuses on the right gives penalties to  意大利 as well as fortifications on certain states.
Rally Around the Emperor Branch
The Monarchist branch will keep Haile Selassie I as the Emperor of Ethiopia.
  • The Emperor Stays Sub-branch
Haile Selassie will remain in Ethiopia to fight the Italians more directly. This sub-branch sees Ethiopia go more independent of the international community. It will then centralize the nation under the Emperor. It allows the formation of the  Empire of Aksum as well as the  Empire of Solomon with expansion in the north and the  Empire of Zion with expansion in the Caribbean.
  • Boarding the Train Sub-branch
While remaining the Ethiopian leader, Haile Selassie will leave the country to directly address the League of Nations to appeal for assistance. It also unlocks the 壮大黑狮会 and the 支援阿尔贝尼奥奇 continuous focuses.
  • An Audience with the Führer Sub-branch ( 德国)
This hidden sub-branch is available when  德国 accepts Ethiopia's appeal for a government in exile in their country. This gives bonuses for German technology and allows Germany to negotiate a peace deal with Italy or worsen relations between Germany and Italy.
  • The American Radio Address Sub-branch ( 英国)
This hidden sub-branch is available when the  英国 accepts Ethiopia's appeal for a government in exile in their country. This gives events that requests land from the British as well as manpower.
  • The American Radio Address Sub-branch ( 瑞典)
This hidden sub-branch is available when  瑞典 accepts Ethiopia's appeal for a government in exile in their country. This gives a free intelligence agency as well as fomenting a revolt in  Libya and asking Sweden to join the war.
  • The Lion and the Sun Sub-branch ( 日本)
This hidden sub-branch is available when  日本 accepts Ethiopia's appeal for a government in exile in their country. This gives Ethiopia a navy from Japan, as well as Japanese help for the war at the cost of being a puppet under them.
  • Support Anti-Colonialist Resistance/Rebuilding the country Sub-branch
After the war, this shared section allows Ethiopia to rebuild, federalize the country so that the other Horn of Africa nations are able to use their focus trees. It also requests  奥萨和 吉布提 to be annexed. In the end, it allows the creation of the African Union faction or the Organization of African Unity and foment the colonial nations to rebel against their overlords.
Reach Out to the Italians Branch
This 法西斯主义 branch will trigger a civil war, but it allows Ethiopia to avoid total annexation and instead become an Italian collaborator. However, it all depends on whether or not the Italians accept or reject the proposal. This allows the country to turn into either in the north as  埃塞俄比亚总督区 under Haile Selassie Gugsa as  Gojjam under Hailu Telke Haymanot, or  Jimma in the south under Abba Jofir.
  • Amedeo's Feast of Maskal Sub-branch
This keeps Ethiopia as a subject of  意大利 and allows the country to gain equipment, designers and territorial arrangements from  意大利.

概述

The Ethiopian conflict commenced with hostilities initiated by Ethiopia against Italy, with Italian military forces already deployed to the Ethiopian border. The player will encounter significant challenges in surviving the Italian onslaught, given the limited resources, industrial base, manpower, and equipment available. In order to survive, it is generally accepted that there are two known methods: the first is to attempt to capture Italian-held ports as soon as possible, thereby depriving the enemy of supplies and forcing them to withdraw from the continent. The second method is to attempt to defend the accumulated victory points for as long as possible until external assistance is obtained.

工业

The player will find it difficult to get any kind of construction done as Ethiopia starts with only 1 free civilian factory, so it is recommended that the factory is immediately allocated to importing steel to boost infantry equipment production. Through the national focus tree, Ethiopia can gain more civilian and military factories, which can then be used to import more steel and to produce more infantry equipment. It should be noted that if the player decides to acquire a military factory first, they will find themselves with 0 free civilian factories due to the economic law. It can also be a worthwhile option to assign this factory to build a fort in the state of Addis Ababa if going for the defensive strategy, though after building the fort importing steel is recommended.

国策

Without By Blood Alone, Ethiopia gets the default focus tree, and there are a few different ways to go about using it. Generally, it is recommended to focus on the industry tree to gain more factories. Going down the political tree is also beneficial, as the player can gain extra political power to spend and also extra manpower if going down the Fascist branch. Don't go for the infrastructure focus at the start, though, since it really only gives the Italians the ability to put more divisions on your frontline.

Red alert

If you're playing as 苏联, it's practical to send Volunteer tanks to both help Ethiopia and get 陆军经验. In fact, it's so effective, what it often gets banned in multiplayer - as it's just that debilitating for 意大利.

Air Volunteers will be in numerical disadvantage - as Ethiopia has 2 Air Base levels, meaning only 40 planes could be sent. This is a lot less than amount of italian planes there - and numerical disadvantage will become even bigger once 意大利 starts moving all forces to Ethiopian front in "panic mode" - and even more if 德意志国和日本 send Air Volunteers (Italy has 72 total airfield levels - so, that's not restriction; 德意志国 can send 183 planes, and 日本 can send 174 planes).

苏联 can usually send 6 Volunteer divisions from the start of the game, but can only send 2 to Ethiopia (because it's small) - what allows to send light tank divisions (slightly messed-up ones) or mountaineers (large div with support artillery and engineers). Mountaineers are viable - as it's third world country, with no fuel and lots of mountains; tanks are too - but keep track of fuel.

意大利 forces near Ethiopia are largely colonial divisions - and their templates are completely messed up, with no anti-tank weapons at all. 意大利 only has 3 light tank divisions with equally messed-up templates (it's small, and has only 1 battalion of actual tanks in each) - by the moment volunteers arrive, tanks are already there. Defeating 意大利 in this setup is not easy, but is not overly hard either.

意大利 could send all it's army to Ethiopia, but there would not be sufficient front width for all those units to engage (maybe, some additional units from south) - and such large stacks would eat supplies, in already poorly-supplied region. On top of that, AI forgets what he can send everyone there. Even still, 意大利 has numerical advantage.

On the other side of the conflict: 德意志国 can send 1 light tank division, and 日本 can send 3 light tank divisions. Or, rather 必须 send, especially if 苏联 sent his volunteers. Not only that would give army experience, but 意大利 is known to fail in Ethiopia even without 苏联 intervention - so, there's real need to save your ally from loosing.

Capture the ports

With the correct opening troop movements, the player may find that it is possible to capture the port at the southern coast. By locking down the east-most unit and attempting to flank around on the east, the Italian AI will reallocate troops and leave an opening in the middle of their line, allowing the player to quickly move units down and capture the port. Once the port is captured, allocate a few units to defend the port and the rest to the northern line. (Note that even though there is no port at Mogadishu at the start, if the Italians recapture that state a port will instantly appear.) At this point, the Italians can still push through on the northern front due to having superior firepower so it is recommended that the player either looks for an opening to capture the port, or buy time with good defence and build up a strong enough force to repel the Italians through conventional battle plans. Once the Italians have been kicked out of the horn of Africa, the player will then be free to do whatever they want.

Pocket Defense

It is recommended to keep the two Italian armies in Eritrea and Somalia separate - in order to do this, the player could immediately create a fallback line which stretches from the Harar victory point to the border with the British in the west. These are all mountain provinces, and the two tiles in the west are positioned behind rivers, which makes them extremely difficult to break but easy to defend. The Italian AI usually focuses on attacking the capital, so rotate troops that have low organisation out and move in units with the full organisation to keep the defence going. If the AI is about to break any tile before your reinforcements could arrive, try using the Last Stand command - the extra few hours it can buy may be the difference between capitulating and surviving. Building a level 1 fort in your capital with your 1-2 available civilan factory is also a decent idea, although it may be better to use them to import steel to maximize weapons production as it is likely that the constant assault wouldn't allow any construction anyway. With only 3 Infrastructure, there should be only 2-3 Italian divisions per tile. Take the army advisors to minimize the impact of their assaults. The AI usually gives up on trying to break your national redoubt by mid-1937.

Now for the counterattack. You should have a ton of army XP by now, so invest in creating a division template with line and support artillery (which you need to research); a standard 7/2 template does the job. Gradually convert your weaker divisions into these offensive divisions, and make sure you have enough manpower either by upping your conscription law or by going down the Nationalism branch in your focus tree. Superior Firepower should be your doctrine here, since Italy has more manpower than you and your only advantage relies on breaking their front before they can reinforce it. Once you could mass around 7-8 divisions with artillery, and have sufficient manpower and equipment, you can strike them - usually by early to mid 1938 you have enough equipment for an offensive.

There are two directions you can strike from, north and south. Eritrea only has a single port, so if you capture it there will no longer be any reinforcements and the Italian divisions in the north will start to lose supply. Immediately launch an assault with all your divisions that are not holding the southern front and annihilate their units. Leave a single division guarding Asmara (the AI tends to attempt naval invasions to recapture it), and use your offensive divisions against the Italians in the south. Typically, the easternmost hill tile is the easiest to break compared to the other mountain tiles. Try to encircle the Italian divisions again, and once you've accomplished this it's typically straightforward to push any remaining Italians to the sea and seize the two ports.

Once you've captured every tile, you will be able to click on the decision that will force Italy into a peace with you, leaving you with control over Eritrea and Italian Somaliland. Congratulations, you are now an independent country at peace!

Late game

The player can release  厄立特里亚和 索马里苏丹国 (or form collaboration governments if 抵抗运动 is also enabled). These two countries can gain more factories from their focus trees, and they can be reannexed later if the player wishes.

Once at peace, Ethiopia can choose to join the Axis or the Allies (or the Comintern, for that matter). Joining the Allies would give Ethiopia an opportunity for revenge against Italy, while joining the Axis may net Ethiopia much territorial gain in Africa.
