For game editions, expansions, and flavor packs see 可下载内容.
HoI4的 DLC 界面。

可下载内容（DLC） is content built by Paradox 开发工作室 (PDS) as an extension or add-on to 钢铁雄心 IV. They are modular in nature,[1] which means that a player can choose to play with or without a given DLC by checking them out at the launch menu.

The content described in this page is compromised of music and cosmetic DLCs which may serve to enhance the flavor of the game. Additionally, e-books are available for reading outside of the game. These DLCs do not affect gameplay in any form and are not shared in multiplayer games (as opposed to gameplay affecting content).

Music packs add new music created after the game's release to the game's soundtrack.

  • Katyusha: A pre-order bonus for the Soviet Union themed expansion No Step Back, this is an original arrangement of a Soviet wartime hymn to the patriotic sacrifices made on the eastern front.
  • Bella Ciao: A pre-order bonus for the Italian focused expansion By Blood Alone, “Bella Ciao” became a popular Italian anti-Fascist anthem during the war years.
  • Säkkijärven Polkka: A pre-order bonus for the Nordic themed expansion Arms Against Tyranny, this Finnish folk song became popular on the frontlines of the Winter War, with its chords used as a means of disrupting Soviet radio mines.
  • Carnaval Do Brasil: A pre-order bonus for the South American themed expansion Trial of Allegiance, this is a pastiche of military brass and traditional Brazilian party melodies.
  • Ride of the Valkyries: A pre-order bonus for the German and Central European themed expansion Götterdämmerung, this familiar excerpt from Wagner’s Ring Cycle opera Die Walküre has become synonymous with the threat of war.
