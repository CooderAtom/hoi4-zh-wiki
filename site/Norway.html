 挪威 is a minor power in Northern Europe with a population of 3.09 M. It shares a land border with  瑞典和 芬兰 to the east.

历史背景

Norway attempted to stay neutral in World War II (like all other Nordic Countries) and remained so until the 9th of April 1940, when  德国 invaded both  丹麦 and Norway as part of Operation Weserübung. Operation Weserübung was intended to secure access to Norway's coastline and ports for the Kriegsmarine to use as well as ensuring the safe transportation of Swedish iron ore to Germany. In 1942, Germany established a puppet regime in Norway, known as the Nasjonal Regering or Quisling Regime, headed by Vidkun Quisling, party chairman of the 民族统一党 political party, Norway's fascist party. After the death of Adolf Hitler, the Flensburg Government (successor state to Nazi Germany) drew up plans to withdraw the remnants of the Wehrmacht into Norway and Denmark, establishing those countries as a national redoubt and last holdout against the Allies. Norway remained under German occupation until May 8, 1945.

主条目：Norwegian national focus tree

 挪威 gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 反抗暴政 expansion. Without the expansion, Norway utilizes the 通用国策树.

The Norwegian national focus tree can be divided into 9 branches and 11 subbranches:

A Resurgent Labor Movement Branch
This focus starts the 共产主义 branch of the focus tree.
  • Compromise with the NKP Sub-branch
The Stalinist sub-branch befriends the  苏联 and helps in their war with  芬兰
  • The Seeds of Revolution Sub-branch
The shared 共产主义 sub-branch launches a civil war, removes the King from the country and changes Norway's flag depending if the player goes Stalinist, Trotskyist or otherwise.
  • Prosecute the NKP Sub-branch
The alternative 共产主义 sub-branch goes against the  苏联 and defends  芬兰 in the winter war.
  • Consolidate Power Sub-branch
The shared 共产主义 民主主义 sub-branch gives war goals against  瑞典和 丹麦 in order to prepare for war against the  德意志国. If Norway is democratic, the build relations with other democratic countries and creates the 北欧理事会 faction.
Vote of No Confidence Branch
The alternative 民主主义 branch builds an aggressive Norway to protect it from foreign threats and joins the Allies.
  • Weathering the Storm to Come Sub-branch
The shared 民主主义 sub-branch builds Norway's defenses and prepares it for war.
  • Replace Laake Sub-branch
This shared 民主主义 sub-branch arrests 维德孔·吉斯林, removes the possibility of a civil war from the 法西斯主义者 and gives bonuses to military technology.
  • Forsvarets Intelligence Service/The King's No Sub-branches
Forsvarets Intelligence Service requires 抵抗运动 and gives an intelligence agency. The focuses under The King's No prepares a government in exile after it gets capitulated and prepares it to retake Norway.
Continue Nygaardsvold's Government Branch
The historical 民主主义 branch cuts military industry in favor of civilian industry with the The Broken Gun spirit.
The Stumbling Storting Branch
This focus starts the 法西斯主义和Monarchist branch of the focus tree.
  • Lurk in the Shadows Sub-branch
This section of the 法西斯主义 sub-branch manually builds fascist support in the country and asks for help from other fascist countries.
  • Quisling's Coup Sub-branch
This sub-branch is available under the Historical 民主主义 or the 法西斯主义 sub-branch. It starts a 法西斯主义 civil war and puts 维德孔·吉斯林. Norway can decide to reconquer old viking claims or remove Quisling and become a 傀儡国 of the  德意志国
  • Claims on Denmark/Claims on Sweden Sub-branch
The shared 法西斯主义和Monarchist expansion focuses demands territory from  瑞典,  丹麦, and the  苏联
  • The Royal Chamberlain's Overture Sub-branch
The Monarchist path puts King Haakon VII into power with 佩德·安克尔·韦德尔-亚尔斯伯格 as his regent. It gives expansion opportunities and cores to some of the northern Atlantic Islands and states in the  苏联.
Start Rearmament Branch
The army branch gives bonuses to army technology and modifies the 国家精神 The New Hæren.
Building an Independent Norway Branch
The industrial branch gives industrial bonuses and modifies the 国家精神 An Independent Norway.
Hærens Flyvevaaben Branch
The air force branch builds air bases, gives bonuses to air force technology and modifies the 国家精神 Hærens Flyvevaaben or Luftforsvaret.
Sjøforsvaret Branch

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为挪威建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

The Norwegian Situation

Norway is a minor nation to play, as it is a target of the Third Reich that will eventually annex or puppet them during Operation Weserübung. Despite the naval distance, between these two nations, a scripted civil war that will soon or later will be reinforced, and Norway's obsolete forces debuff makes Norway's position very precarious, as it cannot fight either power on its own. Since the Third Reich is by far the greatest threat to its survival, Norway must seek to ally itself with either the Allies or a domestic faction with Sweden until it can stand on its own two feet.

The Cheese your fighter Strategy

From the start of the game go down the monarchy path. Delete your starting army and airplanes. Somewhere before taking the focus 挪威王国 go down Start Rearmament, Kickstart Military Industry和Expand Hæren. This so you get an starting industry and some extra manpower. 1 factory each, for now, for: Infantry Equipment, Support Equipment, Artillery, Anti-Air, Trucks and Trains. Adding more factories along the way (for example after Shield of the North. Furthermore, there are several things to keep in mind:

  • You need over 55% war support to unlock Shield of the North focus. When you're working on the monarchist demonstration decisions, keep in mind that you should utilize the political power to gain more war support ( The negative construction/civ factory state modifier reduction/removal decision remains available after taking the focus 挪威王国). So you should wait with taking that decision until your war support is at least 55%.
  • You need to take the demonstration decision 12 times to be able to complete the focus 挪威王国. Doing more has no added value, unless you want some cheap militia. But in that case I would advise taking 4 of the demonstration decisions 3 times.
  • Besides the the three military/industry focuses mentioned in the first paragraph every focus until deep in to 1939 will be in the monarchy path.
  • Don't spend any PP on advisors until you are done with the monarchy decisions for war support and removing the construction/civ factory modifier. The only exception is raising your economy law to Partial Mobilization.
  • Create an Spy Agency in time. Cancel trade temporarily for enough civilian factories if needed.

Two Achievements for the price of One Game

As Norway, it has the ability to go for more than one achievement, if followed the correct steps. Therefore, compromising the personal ideas for this gains can be done necessarily.

Pinot Norway

From the start of the game go down the communist side of the tree and choose to "Compromise with the NKP". Delete your starting army and airplanes as with the previous strategy mentioned above. Conserve your political power until you reach "The Seeds of Revolution" and spend it all on the decisions it unlocks. The focus "Organize Partisan Militias" will allow you reach level 3 of the Revolution Decisions and spawns a couple divisions in the states you maxed. If done correctly you should be able to launch the civil war end 1936 and be communist soon after. After that build up your industry (both communist tree and economy tree give a good amount of factories). Justify on Denmark and annex them completely in the Peace Deal. Continue building your army and economy and have a justification for Poland ready for August / early September 1939. When Germany declares war you do the same, a couple in-game hours later Germany allows you to join the Axis. Together Defeat Poland/Benelux/France and then naval invade the UK. In the Peace Deal take Bourgogne and take England and try to get the states in East-Canada that border the USA. After that focus on spending the remainder of your points on the British Fleet.

挪威，何塞（Norway Jose）

After the war with the Allies (or during) justify on Sweden and in the Peace Deal annex them completely. You now control Norway, Denmark, Sweden and England. This allows you to reform the Kalmar Union and more important the far more powerful North Sea Empire. Giving you loads of manpower and factories. Next increase your army to five full armies / 120 divisions. Depending on whether you got part of Canada in the Peace Deal with the Allies or not makes the next step easy or a long longer.

If you did: While building up your army, also improve your Canadian lands (Supply Hubs, etc). Move your army to the USA and justify on Mexico. When you declare on Mexico, they'll call in the USA and in 1941 it should be fairly easy to defeat both.

If you did not: The road ahead is long. Build up your army - > Defeat the Axis (wait until they progressed quite a bit into the SOV) - >Take Canada in the Peace Deal -> Justify on Mexico - > Defeat USA - > Defeat Mexico.

OR: Build up your army -> Justify on China -> Declare war -> Leave the Axis and join the Japanese Faction - > Defeat China together _> Defeat USA together -> Annex borderlands with Mexico -> Justify on Mexico. Alternatively: Depending on the how much of the British Fleet you took you can also try naval invasions. But without back up that can arrive quickly your chances of success might be a quite a bit lower, but it isn't impossible.
