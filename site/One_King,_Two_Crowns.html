一王两冠（One King, Two Crowns）

作为波旁西班牙，持有西班牙和法国的全部核心州。

This 成就 requires  卡洛斯西班牙 controlling all of  法国's cores in Europe.

「Strategy」

注意： This strategy assumes that you play on 补丁 1.9 Husky. Much of the required functionality was introduced in 1.9 / 抵抗运动, and the changes in the subsequent 1.9.1 patch made the achievement much harder to get. You should revert to 1.9 if you plan to follow this strategy guide.

Civil War Prep

Merge fleet and move it to Spanish Africa, pick A Great Spain and continue until Sin Paquito, Move army to metropolitan Spain and research superior firepower

When the election pops up pick nationalists before clicking "Suppress the Strikes"; spam "Suppress the Strikes" until civil war starts. Place Division on fallback line on victory points:

  • 2 division Valencia (167) PLACE YOUR CAVALRY DIVISION HERE
  • 3 divisions Cádiz (1048)
  • 3 divisions Sevilla (7183)
  • 3 divisions Córdoba (875)
  • 3 divisions Málaga (9979)
  • 3 divisions Granada (1176)

Ensure that you only have 1 tick(influence) in states:

  • Sevilla (169)
  • Granada (173)
  • Córdoba (789)
  • Murcia (168)
  • Valencia (167)

内战

Go to Spanish Africa (290) set fleet on naval invasion in Western Mediterranean (68) and set divisions to naval invade victory points:

  • Barcelona (9764)
  • Tortosa (6966)
  • Alícante (4098)
  • Murcia (10024)
  • Almería (12038)

Next head over to Spain proper and select all the division not connected to the front in the north and put them into an army with Mohamed Meziance as commander, reassign their orders if necessary to ensure that the occupied victory points are held.

Finally, head over to the north front and assign division in Galicia (171) and other division close to northern  共和西班牙, this army should have 15 divisions on a frontline against Asturias (790) and País Vasco (792) and micromanage attacks so you seize the ports ASAP. Next for the major front assign all remaining divisions on a front with an offensive line along the 2nd river south of Madrid. Try to pin divisions and move in to occupy Madrid before the Frontlines Solidify event occurs.

In the strategy as originally written (see linked video), this was all there is to it, because Republican Spain capitulated at 40% VPs. Patch 1.9.1 fixes this and now Republican Spain capitulates at 5% VPs (which basically means that you have to take almost every single victory point they have). You can still squash Republican Spain following this general strategy, but, once Frontlines Solidify fires, further action slows down to a crawl (the entire theater is hit with an "Unplanned Offensive" modifier, giving everyone -90% attack penalty and -75% speed penalty). You can clear out the modifier one state at a time by taking the "Preparing Offensive" decision (which is on a 90-day cooldown), and across all northern states by taking "The War in the North" focus. Depending on your luck, you can generally hope to wrap up the war in 3 to 6 months.

Civil War II

Finishing the Republican faction is only half of the problem.

In 1.9, defeating Republican Spain ends the civil war, prevents further uprisings, and lets you bypass focuses up to & including No Compromise on Carlist Ideals.

After 1.9.1, defeating Republican Spain only gives you a breather. When either the timer on "Impending Fascist Crackdown" ticks down to 0 or you take the No Compromise on Carlist Ideals focus (whichever happens first), a fascist uprising is triggered and you'll have to fight them. This will play out similar to the first round, except that you start with less land but your opponents will likely be weaker.

If you wait for the timer to run down (allowing you to take the "Establish Carlist Cell" three times, so that you start the second round with three extra states), the second round will kick off in July 1937. You can try to start it earlier if you want to rush things (this timeline is already far longer than the strategy originally envisioned.)

At the conclusion of the second round, you have a severely weakened country (you only have cores in 7 out of 19 Spanish states on the Iberian Peninsula, core population of ~7M, 2 capital ships, zero compliance in non-core provinces, and possibly as little as 8 accessible civilian factories and 2 naval dockyards.) You can re-core the rest of the peninsula, but this requires taking a 140-day detour down a different branch of the focus tree.

You now have to take 5 70-day focuses (Confirm the Fueros, Settle the Succession Question, Dios, Patria y Rey!, Restore the Empire, Claim the French Throne) to get a casus belli against France. (Carlist Spain is non-aligned and it requires 50% world tension to justify wars).

The rest of the strategy has not been confirmed to work with the latest game, especially since 1.9.1 changes delay the timeline by up to a year; YMMV.

Invasion of France Prep

Immediately after civil war you should start Confirm the Fueros and rush to Claim the French Throne. Train 10 infantry brigades on high priority and research mobile warfare doctrine. Move the fleet to Bilbao (740). Deploy the infantry brigade when at 20% and convert to cavalry, put the cavalry divisions in 1 army and set the following naval invasions:

  • 3 divisions from Bilbao (740) to Calais (11548)
  • 3 divisions from Oviedo (11707) to Dieppe (485)
  • 2 divisions from Oviedo (11707) to Cherbourg (6449)
  • 1 division from Bilbao (740) to Lorient (6536)
  • 1 division from Bilbao (740) to Nantes (11465)

Put the rest of the army on a frontline against  法国 with an offensive line north, except for a couple of units on the border with Gibraltar (since France is in the Allies, declaring on France automatically brings in the UK as well.)

The Invasion of France

Right when Claim the French Throne focus completes declare war on  法国, set your navy on naval invasion support in Bay of Biscay (42) and English Channel (18), activate all orders slowdown game and wait for the first two division to land in Nantes (11465) and Lorient (6536). The division sent to Lorient should be attempting to occupy Brest without any combat, after you have ensured Brest is going to be occupied head over to Northern  法国 however prioritize the northern landings before this as you need to micromanage the northern divisions as soon as they land.

The divisions landing in Cherbourg (6449) will be sent to Rouen (11563) and Paris (11506), The ones in Dieppe (485) will be sent to Rouen (11563), Paris (11506) and Amiens (11420), And finally the divisions in Calais (11548) will be sent to Dunkerque (575), Paris (11506) and Lille (11483).
