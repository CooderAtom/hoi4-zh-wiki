策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为库尔德斯坦建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

House of Kurds achievement

Although unsurprisingly, Kurdistan finds itself split between 4 countries, each which can release the country manually. Although released, it faces several difficulties starting internally with a small population to fend against their neighbor that has their own cores, depending which country releases it. Mainly France and Turkey that can pose as a serious threat if faced against them.

The best choice scenario would be to start off as France. It has more firepower in comparison to its Middle Eastern nation neighbors and Turkey. Take the necessary focuses to declare war on Turkey, Iraq and Persia either in order or selecting from the weakest to the strongest. Take their cores or annex them, and as you do so, release and play as Kurdistan.

Turkey is yet another challenge, arguably the second strongest nation for the picking that starts with army debuffs. With a bit of focus clearance, go for Iraq and Persia. Take their Kurdish cores or fully annex them. Declare war on France next, and join the closest faction (Axis) as France is already a member of the Allies. Assist the Axis members for a naval invasion on the British Isles, and hope for the best to get the last piece in the peace conference, should the Allies capitulate entirely.

Otherwise, you can release them as France, and start it from there. Although it may appear less cored in comparison released by Iraq or Persia, since it it has less population in comparison. On the bright side, it wont have to pick a tiring and long war with the Allies, thus the main big issue would be Turkey. As released Kurdistan, flip fascist (or communist) to make haste to justify on your neighbors Iraq and Persia. Then join the Axis for the campaign against Turkey, as you declare war on them.

This achievement is now possible through the Iraq focus tree with 帝国坟场 enabled if you go down the 推动库尔德认同 path.
