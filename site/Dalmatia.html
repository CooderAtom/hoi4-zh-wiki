 达尔马提亚 is a releasable state of  南斯拉夫. When used with the Yugoslavia Fragmentation Status rule(which can be found in the custom game rules), it borders Slovenia to the northwest, Croatia to the northeast,  波斯尼亚和 黑塞哥维那 and Montenegro to the southeast, and shares a maritime border with Italy to the west.

历史背景

During World War II, in 1941, Nazi Germany, Fascist Italy, Hungary, and Bulgaria occupied Yugoslavia, redrawing their borders to include former parts of the Yugoslavian state. A new Nazi puppet state, the Independent State of Croatia (NDH), was created. With the Treaties of Rome, the NDH agreed to cede to Italy Dalmatian territory, creating the Governatorate of Dalmatia, from north of Zadar to south of Split, with inland areas, plus nearly all the Adriatic islands and Gorski Kotar. Italy then annexed these territories, while all the remainder of southern Croatia, including the entire coast, were placed under Italian occupation. Italy also appointed an Italian, Prince Aimone, Duke of Aosta, as king of Croatia.

国策

通用国策树。
主条目：通用国策树

达尔马提亚 缺少专属国策树，因此改用通用国策树。

通用国策树有5个主要分支：

陆军建设分支
提供陆军经验，并提供陆军科研的科研加成。
航空建设分支
提供空军经验、建造空军基地，并提供空军科研的科研加成。
海军建设分支
提供海军经验、建造海军船坞，并提供海军科研的科研加成。
工业建设分支
建造民用工厂与军用工厂（通常即使建造槽已满也可建造）、基础设施，并给予额外科研槽。
政治建设分支
让国家可以选择要走的政治路线。

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为达尔马提亚建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

Yugoslavia Fragmentation Focus

It's no secret that Dalmatia starts in a stuck position with no resources and army, but given some smart moves and cheese you should be able to form a pretty formidable Dalmatia, given some time, effort, and luck. It comes with compensation of 2 factories, 1 civilian factory and naval factory. Select Yugoslavia, and by focus release Dalmatia and take it from there.

Unlike Herzegovina, Dalmatia doesnt possess a tag of its own. Instead it comes with a civil war tag, and dependent by Yugoslav national focus. Although by console commands, and in Yugoslavia fragmented rule, all you have to do is to bring Yugoslavia from the dead with console commands. Annex Croatia, and with console commands again take the Devolved Croatia focus that allegedly releases them both with infantry units.

Now, the first thing you should do is take the first Political focus, which will give you 120 political power, plus the 70 you have already accumulated. Use this to get a fascist advisor. Yes, I know, going fascist is the go-to strategy for basically every minor, but if it works, it works. While you wait for fascist support to tick up, take some industrial focuses to get some free factories. Grab the two free civilian factories first, then get the three military factories. By this time you should be above fifty percent fascist support, so hold a referendum to become Independent State of Dalmatia. Use your excess political power to get war economy, and if you have any extra left, get some Improved Worker Conditions or Anti-Communist/Democratic raids. Otherwise, if your manpower is still an issue you can go communist to get to the weekly manpower army experience.

By now you should have a total of 7 or 8 factories- 5 civilian and 3 military. One of your civilian factories will be used for consumer goods, so use the remaining one to either build some more civs or trade for some steel. Use one of your 3 military factories to produce artillery, and use the other 2 to build guns. Set some factories to build support equipment as well once you get some more mils.

At this point, you should've flipped fascist already, so start justifying on Montenegro. At the same time start working on the political focuses. Make a template of one cavalry unit, and spam it out. You should also have a fair amount of political power at this point, so take some stability-increasing decisions. Should you declare on Bosnia, Croatia, or god forbid, Serbia and win against them each other can give you enough expansion and access to some valuable resources and sea access for marine trade.

Tip: If you let independent Dalmatia be controlled by an AI, it will annex Croatia by referendum if they share the same ideology. Dalmatia that way will gain additional cores and as well as a bigger army, and inheriting claims from Croatia itself.
