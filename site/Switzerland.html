 瑞士 is a landlocked minor country in Central Europe with a population of 4.22 M. It shares borders with 3 of the game's 7 major powers:  法国 on the west,  意大利 to the south, and  德国 to the north. It also borders  奥地利 to the east.

历史背景

Switzerland has the longest-standing policy of military neutrality in the world, dating back to the Vienna Congress in 1815, where the European coalition powers agreed that Switzerland should be neutral, and the country has remained so ever since.

Despite being landlocked, wedged between three European major powers and located smack-dab right in the middle of a continent ravaged by war, Switzerland stayed neutral and aggressively maintained that neutrality in both world wars, earning it the reputation of being the ultimate neutral country as it was never invaded even once in either war, whether due to successful deterrence, economic concessions and due to bigger things being at stake.

To maintain said neutrality in World War II, however, Switzerland practised a form of gold trade with both the Allies and the Axis (the latter of which surrounded Switzerland) which, in the case of the axis, could be interpreted as a form of money laundering as the gold bought by the Swiss was plundered from national banks across Europe and the swiss francs sold to the Axis were used in commerce with other neutral countries. This, in turn, earned Switzerland something of a reputation of cleverly taking advantage of other peoples' wars for personal profit, with Swiss banking being emblematic for Switzerland, along with chocolate, watchmaking, the Alps and neutrality. Switzerland's neutrality was threatened when Germany considered launching an invasion of Switzerland and Liechenstein with it's allies 意大利和维希法国 in Operation Tannenbaum but this was later abandoned and the country remained safe from war. For all its faults, Switzerland remained a free democratic country throughout the war and was also a rare safe haven for refugees.

An armed neutrality was maintained throughout the Cold War and remains in place to this day by Switzerland not being a member of either NATO or the  欧洲联盟, despite still being surrounded by European major countries and all of the political, economic, diplomatic, geographic and military intrigue that that entails.

主条目：Swiss national focus tree

 瑞士 gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 唯有鲜血 expansion. Without the expansion, Switzerland utilizes the 通用国策树.

The Swiss national focus tree is a single branch that can be divided into 10 sub-branches:

Swiss Guiding Principles Branch
This starts the Swiss focus tree and leads the player to many different paths, including choosing which neighbor;  德国,  意大利 or  法国 Switzerland would start fortifying against.
  • Ban Foreign Nazi Propaganda Sub-branch
This path appeases the 民主主义 neighbor while decreasing opinion from 法西斯主义 neighbors.
  • Allied Gold/ Purchase American Planes Sub-branch
This path further increases 民主主义 opinion while pushing Switzerland to no longer be neutral and joins a 民主主义 faction. If it is a chooses to become a puppet of  法国, it will form the  海尔维希亚共和国（法语名）. This also forms the  阿尔卑斯联邦, regardless of which path Switzerland chooses.
  • Gotthardbund Sub-branch
This branch allows Switzerland to expand its borders by gaining claims on other Alpine states. By annexing  Austrian Vorarlberg, Switzerland may also annex Liechtenstein. This also centralizes power, and depending on the ideology of the Confederation President, Switzerland may become 共产主义 or 中立主义. This also forms the  阿尔卑斯保护国 if the President is 中立主义 and forms the  阿尔卑斯联盟 if the President is . In the end, Switzerland may turn back to how it was before, as a 民主主义.
  • A Swiss Navy Sub-branch
If Switzerland borders the ocean, it may get bonuses while creating its navy.
  • Support Allied Espionage Sub-branch
With the 抵抗运动 DLC, Switzerland may use its spy network to help the 民主主义 alliance and may join their side in the war.
  • Promote Henri Guisan/ Frontier Defense Plan Sub-branch
At the cost of opinion with neighboring majors, Switzerland may boost its defensiveness and military capabilities.
  • Federal Police Intelligence Department Sub-branch
By creating an intelligence department, Switzerland may crack down on dissenting ideologies.
  • Purchase German Planes Sub-branch
Switzerland may appease 法西斯主义 neighbors while improving its airforce.
  • Closer Ties with The German Reich Sub-branch
By turning 法西斯主义, Switzerland may choose 提拔托布勒 and stay independent, or choose 提拔亨内 and becomes a  德国 傀儡国, the  瑞士帝国保护国. In the end, it may also form the  阿尔卑斯霸权.
  • Expand Weapons Industry Sub-branch
This branch improves Swiss army and air technology

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为瑞士建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。
  • Keeping good relations with surrounding countries helps to deter an eventual invasion of Switzerland, especially from the German Reich and Fascist Italy.
  • 发展你的工业（民用与军用皆然），这样你就能修建工事，并装备和部署规模更大的陆军与空军，二者都能作为抵御入侵的威慑。
    • 由于游戏并不强制历史玩法，你可以把意识形态改为法西斯主义，并使用通用国策树，从而获得额外+7%可动员人力，同时更自由地通过更极端的征兵法令。
      • At that point, however, there arises the question about whether or not to abandon neutrality altogether and actively join the axis to try and profit off the war and earn a slice of the cake in the form of annexed land or puppets. It is up to the player to decide that.
      • One good example is to join wars with Germany when Germany attacks France, this way Switzerland can gain victory points and have control over some French states. However, watchout for the manpower and equipment required to supply the garrison troops
  • Fortifying the country (especially the borders) by building Land Forts and Anti-Air makes the AI less likely to attack you. At Fort level 6 and above, the AI will straight-up refuse to attack your defensive line.
  • After taking the "Expand the Confederation" focus, by simply maxxing out on political power gain (not taking any focuses and not appointing any advisors), the decisions to promote ideology and referendums in neighboring providences becomes available. This causes your neighboring countries to lose cores and for Switzerland to gain cores on said territories. While this takes forever, and is repetitive, it is a bloodless way to take over nearly the entirety of the eastern hemisphere and gain cores on all controlled territory. (Note: After having an empty political advisor slot there are debuffs to your nation, these debuffs do not stack so it is fine to leave the debuff as is in order to save up some more political power).
