钢铁雄心 IV (or HOI4) is a grand strategy game by Paradox Interactive and the sequel to 钢铁雄心 III. It covers the time period from 1936 to 1948, focusing on the build up to the Second World War and the war itself.

Hearts of Iron IV was announced on the Paradox Forums on the 23rd of January 2014. It was released on D-Day 2016 (2016-06-06)[1].

Game features

  • 全面战略战争：战争不只在陆上、海上和空中取胜，还要赢得人心。
  • 逼真的实时战争模拟：让二战中最伟大的指挥官用当时的工具为你作战——坦克、飞机、舰船、枪炮以及新发现的毁灭性武器。
  • 掌控任意国家：选择力争胜利的列强，或选择试图在风暴中求生的小国。
  • 把世界变成你的战场：在一张完整呈现季节、天气与地形的地形图上体验二战全程。冰雪、泥泞和风暴既可能是你的强大盟友，也可能是无情的敌人。
  • 谈判或强加你的意志：体验深度的政治与外交系统，组建阵营，进行资源贸易，并为你的党派任命部长。
  • 激烈的在线战斗：在最多 32 名玩家的竞技与合作多人对战中作战。支持中途加入与跨平台联机。
  • 让你的国家独具优势：体验灵活可变的科技系统，每个主要国家都拥有独特的特色。通过科研和陆军经验研发细节丰富的历史坦克与飞机。

Development team

The game was developed by a core team of six, augmented by drop-in support from the rest of Paradox Development Studios.

  • Podcat (lead/design/programming)
  • Darkrenown (script/research/design)
  • TomaszKowalczyk (programmer)
  • Guraan (programmer)
  • Sideburnout (2D art)
  • larre (3D art/map)

    • Memory: 4 GB RAM
    • Graphics: Nvidia® GeForce™ GTX 470 (1.28GB) | AMD® HD 5850 (1GB) | Intel Iris Xe G7 (Tiger Lake) | AMD® Radeon™ RX Vega 11
    • DirectX: Version 9.0c
    • Storage: 2 GB available space
    • Sound Card: Direct X- compatible soundcard.
    • Additional Notes: Mouse and keyboard are required. Latest available WHQL drivers from manufacturers. Internet Connection or LAN for multiplayer.

    • Processor: Intel Core i5 @ 2.60 GHz or better
    • Memory: 4 GB RAM
    • Graphics: ATI Radeon HD 6970M
    • Storage: 2 GB available space
    • Additional Notes: Mouse and keyboard are required. Latest available proprietary drivers. Internet Connection or LAN for multiplayer.
