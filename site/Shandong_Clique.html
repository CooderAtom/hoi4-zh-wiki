The  鲁系 is a Chinese warlord state in northeastern China with a population of 38.42 M. It borders  中国 to the south, and  冀察 to the west and north.

历史背景

Shandong's return to Chinese administration came during the Warlord Era of the Republic of China, when the Zhili clique of warlords was given control, but after the Second Zhili–Fengtian War of 1924, the northeast China-based Fengtian clique took over. In April 1925, the Fengtian clique installed the warlord Zhang Zongchang, nicknamed the "Dogmeat General", as military governor of Shandong Province. Time dubbed him China's "basest warlord". He ruled over the province until 1928 when he was ousted in the wake of the Northern Expedition. He was succeeded by Han Fuju, who was loyal to the warlord Feng Yuxiang but later switched his allegiance to the Nanjing government headed by Chiang Kai-shek. Han Fuju also ousted the warlord Liu Zhennian, nicknamed the "King of Shandong East", who ruled eastern Shandong Province, hence unifying the province under his rule.

In 1937 Japan began its invasion of China in the Second World War, which would eventually become part of the Pacific theater. Han Fuju was made Deputy Commander in Chief of the 5th War Area and put in charge of defending the lower Yellow River valley. However, he abandoned his base and about 600,000 Ethnic Chinese civilians fled out Jinan city with only elderly too old to leave remaining when Japanese troops crossed the Yellow River.

国策

鲁系国策树。
主条目：Warlords national focus tree

The  鲁系 uses the shared Warlords national focus tree along with the other warlords. It also gets access to the new shared Nationalist和共产主义 focus trees for the warlords at the end of the tree.

The Shandong Clique national focus tree can be divided into 2 branches and 3 sub-branches:

确保内部政治分支
该分支开启军阀的政治国策。
  • 与国民党合作子分支
该分支允许军阀与国民党中国结盟。这会把当前国策树替换为国民党军阀国策树。
  • 与共产党合作子分支
该分支允许军阀与中国共产党结盟，并能在获得苏联支持后取代并吞并后者。当取代中华苏维埃共和国时，当前国策树将变为中国共产党军阀国策树，其所有国家精神都将归你所有。
  • 反对派子分支
该分支允许军阀与其他中国势力挑起边境冲突，与日本结盟，然后建立一个不同于Nationalist和中国共产党、与二者对立的政府，并根据政党支持度给予其国民党军阀国策树或中国共产党军阀国策树。
发展首都州分支
这是国策树的工业分支。
强化军阀权威分支
该分支会修改国家精神Warlord State，并为该国的骑兵或山地兵提供加成。

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为鲁系建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

Playing as  鲁系 is very hard, the first problem that needs to be addressed is the player's almost total lack of industry.  鲁系 actually holds the title for having the lowest starting industry out of any nation in the game (not counting any releasable nations). To which it shares it with  蒙疆,  甘马（马家军）和 和田马家军.

Pick "Develop Jinan" as the first focus. Immediately rush it down until you get everything necessary to produce and build anything. Your goal will be to declare and unite all of the Chinese nations one by one, even sooner depending if Japan decides to go with its fascist branch or not. Take "Secure Internal Politics" and go for opposition, in order to go down and produce and get more manpower out of it. Pick a few decisions to improve on Han Fuju's traits, as having the title as 'Unreliable Opportunist' isn't a fitting title to lead the entirety of China.

If the player wants to do the other part, make sure you prepare yourself to fight China and the rest of the warlord nations, the player only has to worry about Japan keeping its word to have you in their faction, in order to go as far as unite all of China. Fighting the Chinese can be relatively tedious. Shanxi and Hebei Chahar being relatively easy to take down with the outmaneuvering strategy. And China would be a difficulty as they have more territory, strength in numbers and factories. If the player has finally taken China with the Japanese help, make sure that they do the most of effort to leave little to no lands to give to Japan. Finally with the Chinese United Front being dealt with, by using the same outmanoeuvring and pin down attack strategies, Shandong Clique can take any side and core between Communist, Nationalist Chinese focuses. With this done and if the player has completed all of the warlord focuses of Shandong Clique, it is time to rush and go for Warlord subjugation in the generic Nationalist China focus tree. It would give a few wargoals for the player to strike and annex the warlords. Alternatively, go with the "Invite Foreign Investors" by rushing down all the way until you reach the "One China Policy".

Considering that the "Renegotiate the Unequal Treaties" focus is usually bypassed as Shandong Clique doesnt have the national spirit. If the Ma Cliques and the rest of the West sided warlords aren't yet at war with Japan, it is a good opportunity to pick targets one by one. Be vary of the territories. Of the said nations. The Northeastern Army, in comparison to Sichuan and Communist China, is an easier target. Whereas the other two tend to increase their army, and utilize their mountainous terrain. In alternative to that, use the border conflict decision missions as an alternative to gain territory against the neighbors. Make sure to fix the supply routes, research trains and improve on division templates. Otherwise, if that proves to be obsolete, simply declare war on them by "One China Policy" focus, battle them in a preferable terrain. Or, research mountaineer divisions to fare better in the mountainous battlefields.

In addition, Shandong Clique can ironically become the second most industrial power in China, only behind Chiang's Flag of China China, thanks due to both its Warlord and Chinese shared focus trees.
