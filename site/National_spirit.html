在政治菜单中，当前的国家精神显示在领袖肖像右侧。

A 国家精神 is a modifier that describes unique advantages or disadvantages of countries. A country can have several national spirits at the same time. They can be acquired, removed, or modified by finishing 国策 or through certain events. National spirits can be viewed in the political menu. Some countries already start with national spirits as can be seen below.
