比属刚果 or as known as Democratic Republic of Congo (扎伊尔) is a minor nation in Africa. They start out as an colony of  比利时. It has the releasables of  布隆迪,  卢旺达和 加丹加国. It is bordered by  法国,  比利时,  英国和 葡萄牙. Alternatively, when used with the Africa Decolonized rule (which can be found in the custom game rules), it is bordered by the  Republic of the Congo,  中非共和国,  苏丹,  乌干达,  卢旺达,  布隆迪,  坦桑尼亚 (across Lake Tanganyika),  赞比亚,  安哥拉,  加丹加国, the Cabinda exclave of Angola, and the South Atlantic Ocean. Most of it is dense jungle while the state of Leopoldville is mostly forest. Although not in a great position, it has the possibility to greatly expand.

历史背景

The Belgian Congo was directly involved in the two world wars. During World War I (1914–1918), an initial stand-off between the Force Publique and the German colonial army in German East Africa turned into open warfare with a joint Anglo-Belgian-Portuguese invasion of German colonial territory in 1916 and 1917 during the East African campaign. The Force Publique gained a notable victory when it marched into Tabora in September 1916 under the command of General Charles Tombeur after heavy fighting.

During World War II, the Belgian Congo served as a crucial source of income for the Belgian government in exile in London after the occupation of Belgium by the Nazis. Following the occupation of Belgium by the Germans in May 1940, the Belgian Congo declared itself loyal to the Belgian government in exile in London. The Belgian Congo and the rest of the Free Belgian forces supported the war on the Allied side in the Battle of Britain with 28 pilots in the RAF (squadron 349) and in the Royal South African Air Force (350 Squadron) and in Africa. The Force Publique again participated in the Allied campaigns in Africa. Belgian Congolese forces (with Belgian officers) notably fought against the Italian colonial army in Italian East Africa, and were victorious in Asosa, Bortaï and in the Siege of Saïo under Major-general Auguste-Eduard Gilliaert during the second East African campaign of 1940–1941. On 3 July 1941, the Italian forces (under General Pietro Gazzera) surrendered when they were cut off by the Force Publique. A Congolese unit also served in the Far Eastern Theatre with the British army in the Burma campaign.

During the 1940s and 1950s, the Belgian Congo experienced extensive urbanisation and the colonial administration began various development programs aimed at making the territory into a "model colony"

国策

比属刚果国策树。
主条目：Belgian Congolese national focus tree
主条目：Congo joint national focus tree branches

 比属刚果 gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 诸神黄昏 expansion. Without the expansion, The Belgian Congo becomes the Congolese state, is releasable and utilizes the 通用国策树.


The Belgian Congolese national focus tree can be divided into 9 branches, 8 sub-branches and 1 joint branch section:

Belgian Congo Branch
The joint branch shared with  比利时. It mainly builds industry within the Congo region, builds up the Force Publique, and sets the future of the  比属刚果.
Congolese Businessowners Branch
This decreases and removes the effects of the 国家精神 The Invisible Roof. It can only be done when the  比属刚果 is independent.
Governor-General Pierre Ryckmans Branch
This pre-independence branch strengthens the colonial regime. It builds up the country's industry, develops certain states and decreases autonomy.
  • The Intore Lion Dancers/Formally Recognize the Kimbanguist Church Sub-branch
These shared focuses give units and a special advisor that increases autonomy and compliance.
Found the Congolese National Movement/National Committee for Liberation Branch
This pre-independence branch pushes the Belgian Congo to independence. The left side focuses on a more diplomatic independence, while focusing on increasing 民主主义 popularity. The right side focuses on a more violent independence via civil war with mainly increasing 共产主义 or 法西斯主义 popularity and getting aid from either  德国 or the  苏联
The Congo and the War in Europe Branch
If  比利时 ends up in a war and the Belgian Congo is its puppet, this branch becomes available. It gives resources to its allied nations like  英国和 南非, gains supplies from the  美国 and it may be able to gain independence with  英国 backing.
Establish the Armée Nationale Congolaise Branch
The military branch modifies the 国家精神 Armée Nationale Congolaise and builds up the navy, airforce and army.
Kasa-Vubu's First Cabinet Branch
This post-independence branch is available if the country has more than 40% 民主主义 popularity. It lowers the effects of An Uncontrollably Vast Nation, and builds the country's industry. It grants claims to the Congo Basin.
  • Nationalize Foreign Industrial Assets Sub-branch
This shared post-independence branch builds up industry, gains resources, and gives bonuses to infrastructure, and supply construction.
The Lion Roars Branch
This post-independence branch is available if the country has more than 40% 共产主义 popularity. It proclaims the People's Republic of the Congo. The left side, allies itself with the  苏联 and builds resistance in colonial territories in Africa. The right side, similar to the branch  埃塞俄比亚 tree, liberates and integrates African countries, and forms the  African Union.
Assemble a Regency Council Branch
If the Congo cannot form a government after independence with less than 40% of either party popularity, a regency council is formed in order to elect a monarch for the country. This will unlock one of six different sub-branches depending on the monarch chosen.
  • The House of Água Rosada Sub-branch
This installs Pedro VII and forms the  Kingdom of Kongo. The newly reformed kingdom may force an independence war or a civil war in the south in order to annex a released  安哥拉 and core the neighboring states. The player may decide to become a Catholic kingdom for defensive and 稳定度 bonuses and increased opinion with other Catholic dominated countries, or it could follow the Bundu dia Kongo religion and gain offensive and 战争支持度 bonuses. It can also reform the royal council and gain military bonuses and 战争目标 justification bonuses. It also has an extra lowering of the effects of An Uncontrollably Vast Nation.
  • The Nyim of Kuba Sub-branch
This installs Mbóp Mábíínc maMbéky and forms the  巴库巴王国. It emphasizes the area's traditional culture and gives industry, construction, population and trade bonuses. It also decreases division attack and defense but increases organization.
  • The Mâ Loango Sub-branch
This installs Moe Poaty III and forms the  罗安戈王国. The country may decide to give absolute power to the King, giving 政治点数和稳定度 or appointing a royal council, decreasing 政治点数 in exchange for 战争支持度, more 稳定度 and advisor cost reduction. It also gives 橡胶 production and a bonus in factory output and breakthrough. For territorial expansion, it allows the annexation and coring of the historical territory of Loango: the  法国 state of the Middle Congo and the  葡萄牙 state of Cabinda. The country may also decide to ally with those countries.
  • The Mwami of Burundi Sub-branch
This installs Mwambutsa IV Bangiricenge and forms the  布隆迪王国. If the state of Burundi was lost, the country can demand the state from its owner. The king can then gain 战争目标 justification bonuses. Burundi may also claim, demand and core states from around the great lakes to rebuild the borders of the Empire of Kitara. It can then expand south to Malawi and Zabezia-Moçambique.
  • The Umwami of Rwanda Sub-branch
This installs Mutara III Rudahigwa and forms the  卢旺达王国. If the state of Rwanda was lost, the country can demand the state from its owner. Rwanda can then expand across the Lake Victoria strategic region and can either choose to develop its industry or build up its military. It can also form the  East African State.
  • The Pro-German Mwami Sub-branch
This installs the pro-German former King of Rwanda Yuhi V Musinga and forms the  中非王国. Unlike the other monarchist sub-branches, this may be done as a 法西斯主义 nation as well as 中立主义. It may then request to become a  德国 protectorate if  德国 is 中立主义 or 法西斯主义 ;or if  德国 hasn't completed the focus Monarchist Sentiment, it may instead take German advisors. Afterwards, It may gain decisions to integrate states within the German planned Middle Africa and states within the same region. It will also gain War Goals against countries that owns those states except the  Portugese colonies.

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为比属刚果建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

Belgian Congo with Africa Decolonized (without the Götterdämmerung DLC)

It's no secret that Belgian starts in a pretty bad position with small resources and a worse industry than Bhutan, but given some smart moves and cheese you should be able to form a pretty formidable Belgian Congo, given some time, effort, and luck.

Now, the first thing you should do is take the first Political focus, which will give you 120 political power, plus the 70 you have already accumulated. Use this to get a fascist advisor. Yes, I know, going fascist is the go-to strategy for basically every minor, but if it works, it works. While you wait for fascist support to tick up, take some industrial focuses to get some free factories. Grab the two free civilian factories first, then get the three military factories. By this time you should be above fifty percent fascist support, so hold a referendum to become Central African Empire. Use your excess political power to get war economy, and if you have any extra left, get some Improved Worker Conditions or Anti-Communist/Democratic raids. This will improve your stability.

By now you should have a total of 4 or 5 factories- 2 civilian and 3 military. One of your civilian factories will be used for consumer goods, so use the remaining one to either build some more civs or trade for some steel. Use one of your 3 military factories to produce artillery, and use the other 2 to build guns. Set some factories to build support equipment as well once you get some more mils.

At this point, you should've flipped fascist already, so start justifying on Congo-Brazzaville and Central African Republic. At the same time start working on the political focuses. Make a template of one cavalry unit, and spam it out. You should also have a fair amount of political power at this point, so take some stability-increasing decisions and maybe a Captain of Industry. Should you declare on Chad, Gabon, Equatorial Guinea, Cameroon, Angola, Zambia, Namibia or god forbid, Niger or Nigeria and win against them each other can give you enough expansion and access to some valuable resources and sea access for marine trade. It won't make you a superpower, but it'll give you a viable chance to expand, and possibly take the entirety of Africa.
