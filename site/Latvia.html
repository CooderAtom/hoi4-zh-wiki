 拉脱维亚 is a minor nation located in Northern Europe with a population of 1.96 M. Located on the coast of the Baltic Sea, it borders  爱沙尼亚 to the north,  立陶宛和 波兰 to the south, and the  苏联 to the east.

历史背景

Latvia achieved independence as a result of the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk, signed between Imperial Germany, Austria-Hungary and the Russian Provisional Government in March 1918. After the collapse of the Russian Provisional government and the rise of the Bolshevik communist regime, the Petrograd Soviet, seeking to prevent Baltic independence and spread the proletarian world revolution, invaded the Baltic region. Latvia joined forces with the rest of the Baltic States to defend its independence and was able to stop the Russian offensive. A peace treaty was signed in 1920 and Latvia's independence was recognized by the rest of the world after World War I. As part of the secret protocol of the Molotov-Ribbentrop pact, Eastern Europe was divided into spheres of interest by  德国 and the  苏联. Latvia was in the Soviet zone and was occupied by the Soviet Union in 1940. This occupation was briefly interrupted in 1941 by Operation Barbarossa when Germany occupied the area, reorganizing the Baltic States into the "Reichskommisariat Ostland". The area was re-occupied by the Soviet Union in 1944-1945 and the Baltic States were annexed into the Soviet Union after the end of World War II and reorganized into the Estonian SSR, Latvian SSR and Lithuanian SSR. Latvia remained part of the Soviet Union until 1991 when it declared itself independent during the collapse of the Soviet Union.

国策

拉脱维亚国策树。
主条目：Latvian national focus tree

 拉脱维亚, along with the other Baltic States, gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 一步不退 expansion. Without the expansion, Latvia utilizes the 通用国策树.

The Latvian national focus tree can be divided into 3 branches and 3 sub-branches:

Rejoin the Railways Branch
This shared Baltic branch upgrades the industry and military of Latvia. The left side requires investment from a 法西斯主义 or 民主主义 major.
Restore the Worker's Republic Branch
This shared Baltic branch allows Latvia to turn 共产主义 and either become a  苏联 puppet or independent. It also allows expansion to the other Baltic states and Scandinavia.
Suspend the Constitution of Latvia Branch
This allows Latvia to modify the Saeima, the Latvian Parliament, to 民主主义 or 中立主义/Militarist. It also allows Latvia to become the Latvian Military Directorate if the military dictatorship path is chosen.
  • Formalize the Baltic Entente/ Beyond the Baltic Sub-branch
This shared Baltic branch allows Latvia to ally with the other Baltic countries and gives bonuses to manpower and industry to all allied Baltic country and decide whether or not to unite the Baltic States. It also allows Latvia to ally the United Kingdom or any other 民主主义 faction.
  • Empower the Paramilitarists Sub-branch
This allows Latvia to either go further 民主主义 or follow a 法西斯主义 path. The 法西斯主义 path allows Latvia to go against, or ally  德国, or go alone. It makes Dievturība the state religion and allows the creation of either  东方总督辖区 or the  波罗的会议.
  • The Threat on Our Border Sub-branch
This allows Latvia to choose which country is the a threat then build up an industry and army after it has capitulated or become a subject to the enemy nation.

地理

Latvia is in the Baltics, which is part of Northern Europe. Latvia's terrain is a mix of forests and plains with its capital city counted as urban terrain. It is made up of five states: Vidzeme, Latgale, Zemgale, Rīga and Kurzeme. They have a land border with Estonia, Lithuania, Poland and the Soviet Union.

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为拉脱维亚建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

Latvia starts off with little Manpower but just enough to make 10 Units. Latvia has the Generic Focus Tree, so for your focuses, select Political Effort and continue down. For Research, you can Research the Normal. Next, you want to go either Fascist or Communist and once you're done, Start Justifing a Wargoal on 爱沙尼亚 and then proceed to take them over. Once you finish conquering the Baltics, Justify a wargoal and attack 波兰. At the Peace Conference, you can either Annex all of 波兰 or puppet them. As well as Sataliting 乌克兰和白俄罗斯. Then you can proceed to go after 罗马尼亚和捷克斯洛伐克. Once you're done with that, you can go attack 匈牙利 (as long as they don't join the Axis or get guaranteed by the Allies.) 南斯拉夫, 希腊, 意大利, 德国, 保加利亚, 丹麦, 芬兰, 土耳其, 伊拉克, Etc. Once you're done conquering Eastern Europe, you can go ahead and attack the 苏联 and take them over. You can then proceed to do a World Conquest after. Latvia also has the option to unite The Baltics. So you can also do that as well.
