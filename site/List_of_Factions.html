阵营是彼此结盟的民族国家所组成的联合体。

Most countries can acquire the ability to form factions through their national focus tree. In general, these have no default name, with the player choosing a name at creation. Several specific factions, inspired by counterfactual history, do have pre-set names, however, and are listed below.

The first list will show all the factions currently createable in-game and mention which template they use.

Factions are created from pre-designed templates. The template contains the starting manifesto. rules and goals a faction will have.

The second list will show each of these templates only once. For example: the Portugese "人民阵线集团" template is also used by the "英国共产主义替代方案", so the two factions are identical, except for the founding members.
