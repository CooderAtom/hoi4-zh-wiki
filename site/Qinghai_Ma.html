Without the 不妥协，不投降 DLC, 青马（马家军） starts as 西北三马, with a population of 12.39 M, owning all states of Gansu Ma and Ningxia Ma, as well as one state from the Northeastern Army and the Sichuan Clique respectively.

 青马（马家军） is a Chinese warlord state in northwestern China with a population of 2.59 M. It borders  甘马（马家军） to the northeast, the  东北军 to the east, the  川系 to the southeast,  西藏 to the southwest, and  和田马家军 to the northwest. Ruled by Ma Lin, it is part of the Ma Clique together with Gansu Ma, Khotan Ma, and  宁马（马家军）.

历史背景

The Ma Clique warlords were all Generals in the military of the Republic of China, who controlled all of Mainland China until it was overtaken by the communist People's Liberation Army. The clique was begun by Muslim generals who served in the military of the Qing dynasty, most notably in the Kansu Braves army, who fought in the Boxer Rebellion against invading foreign forces. It was continued by two generations of their descendants. During the Second Sino-Japanese War, Ma Clique forces fought against the Japanese and later Ma Bufang leader of the Ma Clique was ordered by the Kuomintang to invade  新疆 in the 1940s to intimidate and help oust the forces of the pro Soviet Governor Sheng Shicai. The Ma Clique forces also clashed with the forces of the Second East Turkestan Republic during the Ili Rebellion.

决议

As one of the Ma cliques, Qinghai Ma can attempt to form  西北三马 by conquering the requisite territories of the other Ma Warlords.

国策

青海马家国策树。
未启用不妥协，不投降的军阀国策树。
Main articles: Warlords national focus tree, Ma Clique joint national focus tree branches
主条目：军阀国策树（基础游戏）

 西北三马 uses the shared Warlords national focus tree along with the other warlords and may inherit the  Nationalist or  共产主义 Chinese focus trees. With the 不妥协，不投降 expansion enabled, the Warlords national focus tree is expanded, along with a unique branch only available to the now-called  青马（马家军）, as well as a branch shared with the other Ma warlords. It also grants access to the new shared Nationalist和共产主义 focus trees for the warlords at the end of the tree.

The Qinghai Ma national focus tree can be divided into 2 branches and 3 sub-branches and 2 country specific branches:

确保内部政治分支
该分支开启军阀的政治国策。
  • 与国民党合作子分支
该分支允许军阀与国民党中国结盟。这会把当前国策树替换为国民党军阀国策树。
  • 与共产党合作子分支
该分支允许军阀与中国共产党结盟，并能在获得苏联支持后取代并吞并后者。当取代中华苏维埃共和国时，当前国策树将变为中国共产党军阀国策树，其所有国家精神都将归你所有。
  • 反对派子分支
该分支允许军阀与其他中国势力挑起边境冲突，与日本结盟，然后建立一个不同于Nationalist和中国共产党、与二者对立的政府，并根据政党支持度给予其国民党军阀国策树或中国共产党军阀国策树。
发展首都州分支
这是国策树的工业分支。
强化军阀权威分支
该分支会修改国家精神Warlord State，并为该国的骑兵或山地兵提供加成。


The Military Governor Branch
This branch is exclusive to  青马（马家军）. It represents the power struggle between Ma Lin and Ma Bufang. The player may decide to send Ma Lin to the Hajj thus making Ma Bufang as country leader, or set a diarchy where both lead the country.


Sideline Family Conflicts Branch
该分支为马家军军阀专属。它先作准备，然后要求其他马家军臣服，或对其设定战争目标。接着将其吞并、设为核心州，并接收其将领。这会让组建西北三马更为容易。甘马（马家军）和宁马（马家军）没有各自的专属分支。

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为青马（马家军）建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

主指南

Xibei San Ma starts off with very little resources and little factories. However, they do have a decent amount of manpower as well as 7 units so you can train some up. Choose what focuses you want to do and purchase resources and build up factories. After some time has passed you can attack 新疆, 中华苏维埃共和国, 晋系, and 西藏. You can then wait for the Japanese invasion and attack  中国 along with  日本帝国. You can also join the Japanese faction too. Then you can betray the Japanese and take them over.

与中国统一战线结盟

你可以像之前那样做，只是这次当日本进攻时，加入中国统一战线并向中国请求军事通行权。你和 AI 应当能够击退日本人并赢得战争。

NCNS 指南

Pick "Develop Golog" as the first focus. Immediately rush it down until you get everything necessary to produce and build anything. Your goal will be to declare and unite all of the Ma nations as soon as possible, even sooner depending if  日本 decides to go with its fascist branch or not. Take "Strengthening Our Position" and go for opposition, in order to go down and produce and get more manpower out of it. Pick "Strike at the Traitors" instead of "Demand Submission" as the latter focus will take on forever to integrate and finally form Qinghai Ma as the original  西北三马.

If the player wants to do the other part, make sure you prepare yourself to fight Sinkiang too, the player only has to worry about  共产主义中国 and any of the eastern Chinese nations, in order to go as far as unite all of China. Fighting the Ma's can be relatively tedious. Gansu Ma and Khotan Ma being relatively easy to take down with the outmanoeuvring strategy. Ningxia Ma as well, but it would put a small fight compared to the other two. If the player has finally taken Gansu Ma, go for Khotan Ma, first and then Ningxia Ma second. Khotan Ma falls weak and defeated against the looming war with Sinkiang, so if the player wishes to incorporate cores faster, then Khotan Ma has to go first. Finally with Ningxia Ma last, by using the same outmanoeuvring and pin down attack strategies, Qinghai Ma can form Xibei San Ma. With this done and if the player has completed all of the warlord focuses of Qinghai Ma. Be sure to have completed "The Military Governor Branch" of Qinghai Ma. As you have to declare war on Communist China, Be sure that you've completed "Sweep Out Communists" to buff the attack and defense against them. The territory and front is unfavorable, so you have to lure them out, outmaneuvre them and capture the victory points. Pick the focus of "The Civilian Governor Remains" to get the best spirit, as it helps with supply issues. With all of the warlord focuses finished and now in the generic Nationalist China's focuses, it is time to rush and go for Warlord subjugation in the generic Nationalist China focus tree. It would give a few wargoals for the player to strike and annex the warlords. Alternatively, go with the "Invite Foreign Investors" by rushing down all the way until you reach the "One China Policy".

Considering that the "Renegotiate the Unequal Treaties" focus is usually bypassed as Xibei San Ma doesnt have the national spirit. If China and the rest of the East sided warlords are at war with Japan, it is a good opportunity to pick targets one by one. Be vary of the territories. Of the said nations. The Northeastern Army, in comparison to Sichuan and Communist China, is an easier target. Whereas the other two tend to increase their army, and utilize their mountainous terrain. In alternative to that, use the border conflict decision missions as an alternative to gain territory against the neighbors. Make sure to fix the supply routes, research trains and improve on division templates. Otherwise, if that proves to be obsolete, simply declare war on them by "One China Policy" focus, battle them in a preferable terrain. Or, research mountaineer divisions to fare better in the mountainous battlefields.
