 阿根廷 is a middle-sized country in South America with a population of 16.89 M. It borders  玻利维亚和 巴拉圭 to the north,  智利 to the west,  乌拉圭 to the east and  巴西 to northeast. Argentina's terrain is dominated by plains and deserts. Although it starts out relatively weak, as the largest and arguably second strongest nation in South America besides Brazil, it's in a prime position to expand into its many weaker neighbors and dominate the continent.

历史背景

Leading up to 1936 Argentina had been racked by political instability. Government corruption, failed revolutions and coups, as well as the great depression led to another coup in 1930 establishing a right wing government. José Félix Uriburu, the leader of the coup was later diagnosed with stomach cancer in 1932 leading to elections in which Agustín Pedro Justo came to power. Justo proceeded to begin an interventionist policy to build up the Argentinean economy, industry, and infrastructure. Along with these developments the government maintained a heavy handed approach against dissidents continuing the political turmoil. Argentina declared war on the Axis in March of 1945.

阿根廷国策树。
主条目：Argentinean national focus tree

 阿根廷 gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 效忠审判 expansion. Without the expansion, Argentina utilizes the 通用国策树.

The Argentinean national focus tree can be divided into 5 branches, 3 sub-branches and 1 shared branch section:

A Call to Reform Branch
The 共产主义 branch triggers a civil war in Argentina. It will then lead to either joining the Comintern and keep Rodolfo Ghioldi in power and get  苏联 support or forging another path to get Fanny Edelman in power. Putting Antonio Soto in the Buenos Aires Conference puts an Anarcho-Syndicalist in power and leads to Industrial build up. Keeping Edelman will unite South America under 共产主义.
The Clean Election Branch
The 民主主义 path aligns Argentina with the Allies and decreases the effects of the Infamous Decade national spirit. Argentina may create the Monroe Alliance faction.
  • Join the Allies/Align with the Monroe Doctrine Sub-branch
This sub-branch shared between the 民主主义 and Historical 中立主义 path may allow Argentina to join the Allies, align itself with the  美国 to fight Communists within South America, the  英国 to Fight Fascists and request the British owned South Atlantic Islands, or ally itself with other South American nations and create the Second Argentine Republic.
Roberto María Ortiz Branch
The Historical 中立主义 path installs Roberto Ortiz into power to try and mitigate political corruption. With a threat of a civil war, the player may historically elect Ramón Castillo to try to placate the military, or go non-historical and intervene in states controlled by the military.
  • Work With The Nationalists Sub-branch
The 法西斯主义 path puts the nationalists in power with the help of the  Germans和 意大利人. Argentina may help  国民西班牙 in order to request Equatorial Guinea from them.
  • Dominate the South Sub-branch
This sub-branch gives expansion opportunities via war goal or puppeting in South America and allows the integration of states in the Western Hemisphere.
Fourth Time's The Charm Branch
This hidden branch installs Señor Hilter as leader of Argentina and reconquers and cores  德国 to create the Fourth Reich. This becomes visible when Argentina is 法西斯主义, and a 法西斯主义  德国 with Adolf Hitler as its leader capitulates, An event will appear to put Señor Hilter as leader.
Banco Central de la República Argentina Branch
The Industrial branch builds industry, revisit the British treaties, and promote immigration.


Trial of Allegiance Shared Military Branches
The shared military branches give the nation special national spirits and bonuses for the military. There are special focuses based on which nation the player chooses.

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为阿根廷建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

Argentina's position as a South American country is much more stable than most others, having a small varied supply of resources, such as steel, oil and tungsten (which is enough for most of the game). It can build up its military faster in the early game than other South American countries with less need of imports.

At the start of the game, the player should focus on building up the industry as in any other country. Military factories should be a priority, as Argentina starts with plenty more civilian factories than military. While the building is on course, the player should also expand the military. However it is worth mentioning that most of the terrain of South America is hostile, having a lot of ground covered by hills, forest and mountains will make the use of vehicles a challenge. As such, an 7/2 Infantry artillery are a good choice to do. Companies that help with terrain such as engineer and recon companies will be useful. On the same note, an option that will prove useful with the use of artillery would be the Superior Firepower doctrine branch due to the great amount of soft attack it gives. (Consider that because of the terrain as well as all of South America are incapable of producing effective tank divisions, you'll seldom see armor). Although Mass Assault and Grand Battleplan doctrines also give useful bonuses such as extra manpower and lower infantry width, or extra planning bonus for breaking through the terrain. (Again, most times when attacking you'll have many maluses).

It is recommended that the player starts by going down the industry focus tree, as it will provide civilian, military factories, infrastructure and near the end of it, an additional research slot. Which is needed as Argentina only has 2 research slots.

In addition the player should choose the fascist path of the political part of the focus tree. Since Argentina starts as neutral they don't even have to go fascist first to unlock this path, and can instead opt to keep the rising fascist influence at bay with occasional anti-fascist raids and/or ministers advocating a democratic or communist ideology. That way Argentina can benefit from the massive boosts the fascist path provides, while still being able to pick any ideology it desires.

If the player does decide to go fascist, they can join the Axis and invade the United States by taking over Mexico or through a naval invasion. The same goes for communism just that instead of joining the Axis, you join the Comintern.

Once the player takes over South America they are free to decide how to continue their conquest. It is recommended that by the time they invade the United States (if they go fascist/Communist), it is recommended that they possess a great amount of divisions and have finished the Land doctrines. A strong navy is also recommended as the opposing faction will be a great obstacle in the way of naval invasions.

However if Argentina joins the Allies, your main focus should be taking down the remaining enemies. Although betraying the allies later can also be an option, it's mostly up to the player to decide.
