 爱沙尼亚 is a minor country in Northern Europe with a population of 1.1 M. Located on the coast of the Baltic Sea, it borders  拉脱维亚 to the south, and the  苏联 to the east.

历史背景

As the Russian civil war broke out in November 1917, the Bolsheviks, who had taken over the official government, signed the Brest-Litovsk Treaty, in which they agreed to have a ceasefire with the Germans and ceded parts of western Russia to the advancing German troops. Estonia declared its independence by February 1918. The withdrawal of forces in western Russia after the German surrender in 1918, allowed the Bolsheviks to advance through heading west. Allied forces, in hope of ensuring the independence of Estonia, and hoping to stop the communist advance to the Baltic states, allowed the deployment of the German Freikorps, German anti-communist paramilitaries. The war for independence lasted 14 months and ended with the failure of Red Army troops to conquer the Baltic states and the forced withdrawal of Freikorps from Estonia.

The independence of Estonia lasted for about 20 years. Soviet interests on conquering the region began to increase approaching World War II, and its fate would be decided by the Ribbentrop-Molotov pact, in which Estonia and other Baltic states (among other states and regions) would be included in the Soviet sphere of influence. Estonia was initially forced to allow the Soviets to establish military bases on its territory and was fully occupied by the Soviet Union in 1940, this occupation was briefly interrupted by Operation Barbarossa when Germany occupied the area, reorganizing the Baltic States into the "Reichskommisariat Ostland". The area was re-occupied by the Soviet Union in 1944 and Estonia was annexed into the Soviet Union and reorganized into the Estonian SSR. Estonia remained part of the Soviet Union until 1991 when it declared itself independent during the collapse of the Soviet Union.

国策

爱沙尼亚国策树。
主条目：Estonian national focus tree

 爱沙尼亚, along with the other Baltic States, gets a unique national focus tree as part of the 一步不退 expansion. Without the expansion, Estonia utilizes the 通用国策树.

The Estonian national focus tree can be divided into 4 branches and 3 sub-branches:

Rejoin the Railways Branch
This shared Baltic branch upgrades the industry and military of Estonia. The left side requires investment from a 法西斯主义 or 民主主义 major.
Restore the Worker's Republic Branch
This shared Baltic branch allows Estonia to turn 共产主义 and either become a  苏联 puppet or independent. It also allows expansion to the other Baltic states and Scandinavia.
The Era of Silence Branch
This historical branch gives Estonia military and civilian factories, industrial bonuses and the decision to either turn 民主主义 or to stay 中立主义. It also removes the Vaps threat and gives the possibility to surrender to an invading nation and become its puppet.
  • Formalize the Baltic Entente/ Beyond the Baltic Sub-branch
This shared Baltic branch allows Estonia to ally with the other Baltic countries and gives bonuses to manpower and industry to all allied Baltic country and decide whether or not to unite the Baltic States. It also allows Estonia to ally the United Kingdom or any other 民主主义 faction.
Rally the Nation Branch
This 法西斯主义 branch may trigger a civil war, but it allows Estonia to flip  芬兰 Fascist and later annexing and gaining cores on it. It allows Estonia to gain war goals against the other Scandinavian nations or the  苏联 and either create the  北欧联盟 or create the  爱沙尼亚-芬兰联盟 and later the  Empire of Finno-Ugra.
  • The Threat on Our Border Sub-branch
This gives land forts on border provinces and, depending on which country was chosen as the greater threat, gives attack and defensive bonuses against  德国 or the  苏联.
  • Prepare for the Inevitable Sub-branch
This allows Estonia to build up an industry and army after it has capitulated.

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为爱沙尼亚建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

The Estonian Situation

Estonia is one of the most difficult minor nations to play, as it is a target of the Soviet Union that will eventually annex them. This makes Estonia's position very precarious, as it cannot fight either power on its own. Since the USSR is by far the greatest threat to its survival, Estonia must seek to ally itself with either the Axis or the Allies until it can stand on its own two feet.

Rally the Nation

The path that will follow down to the fascist focus for Estonia. An ambition that goes beyond the Baltics and seeks to unify the Finno Ungrian people under one banner. To start initially, this requires to decision decide every core that has a March on Tallinn completed focus to generate more support for the fascist alignment. It should be made sure that fascist support should go majorly perhaps beyond 83% to secure almost all support on the core states, as the Isamaaliit(non aligned party) will declare a civil war. It should be easy, as it requires to pin down the rest of the troops and go for the undefended capital. Estonia surely lacks in manpower at this point, and it is best advised to complete "The Glory of War" focuses and its rest to generate a decent amount to fill the ranks and have some more in the reserves.

Finno-Baltic Unification

Finland is another nation that the Soviets have a target on too. Although, it is wider and possesses more population, industry and armed forces compared to Estonia, its forces pale in comparison with its neighbours. It is best to utilize the Finnish Militants to march throughout the Finnish cores via decision if Estonia completes "National Pride Marches in Finland". Finland with and without focus tree will subsequently accept annexation following with the "Unite Our Nations" Focus and decisions done. If otherwise, again like with the initial moments of the Tallinn marches, Finland should have a major fascist support in order to make its annexation as swift and easy for the civil war.

What follows next is to unify the Baltics under Estonia. "The Brotherhood" national focus grants annexation goals that favor more than the justification. Latvia being the first to border, possesses 2 submarines that, if it is conquered quickly, can be used as the first gain of a Naval force, albeit a tiny one of that. Including the former Finnish navy, it adds a slight strength to Estonia. Conquering Latvia and Lithuania is maneuverable so it requires to take their capital and the rest of the cities while pinning their troops. In the aftermath of their annexation, form the Baltic Unitary State, which will finally core the rest of the Baltic cores under the Estonian control, including Memel and Vilnius which are controlled by Poland or Germany. The cherry on top is the "Proclaim Finno-Ugria" focus, which allows the nation to be called Estonia-Finland League, gaining cores on all Finland.

The Swedish Conquest

Before heading prematurely to war, a bit of attention should be given on industry, improving supply lines and raising a proper army to make further conquests upon Scandinavia. Some of them may appear weak on paper, but the terrain, supply lines and a possible surprise attack or naval invasion can change the course of action, and turn it in their favor. Put some infantry units in the mainland Baltic states, in order to avoid a quick and humiliating capitulation. And at the same time, add strength on the invading force reserved for Sweden and Norway, especially.

Sweden possesses a decent force, well equipped and can provide itself a solid defense in the harsh terrain. But that wont seem impossible to break through their defenses. Pin most of their troops and let one unit or two to maneuver throughout its territories, and cut them off from supply and rush down to their capital, and soon or later to their last standing core state. Annex them and take the navy. Invading Norway next poses a risk, as it is guaranteed by France and the United Kingdom. The remaining choice is to join the Axis, to prepare for an inevitable war with the Soviets, as they will demand Karjala, and standing against them without the German help will seem difficult.

Anew Winter War

The war with the Soviets will certainly guarantee a large bloodshed, and this means that the manpower will be grinded to dust, trying to defend your soil. Taking Superior Firepower Doctrine will be necessary to be used in this theatre. With Germany on your side, it will help with the heavy lifting, and it will bring the rest of the Axis to help with the campaign. And it will secure the Estonian-Finnish frontlines too, albeit giving more attacks from the flanks other than the German frontline. As the rest of the Axis are doing the work, it is best to built supply lines and hubs extensively to improve the movement and general performance of the advancing Axis army. Push and gain as much victory points from the Soviet, slowly assisting the rest of the Axis to finish the Soviet Union entirely. As they capitulate, take the necessary states to form Finno-Ugria, and depending on the players actions it can take either the rest of the soviet states and other comintern in their possession or just let the rest of the Axis decide what to do.

Road to Finno-Ungria unity

Norway is the remaining country with a sizeable Sami core states. Unless it has joined the Allies or has become an Axis puppet state, the chance of uniting the Finno Ugria nation becomes difficult. If Norway is entirely an Axis member, Estonia-Finland will have to leave the Axis and declare war upon Norway. And, with enough compliance (although it may take a while for 60% compliance), it can finally form Finno-Ugria.

Estonia is Scandinavia

This is an alternative if Estonia has already annexed and cored its Baltic neighbors, and has not annexed Finland yet. And the tensions are relatively mild, Scandinavia possesses another way to be subdued and annexed by Estonia itself. Initially starting with Denmark, depending on its national spirit or path, it will surrender or try to resist. Norway is the next target and it isnt guaranteed yet by the allies, and it best as followed to annex or puppet them along with Denmark. Sweden being the last nation, just like with the Swedish Conquest of the first strategy can be invaded this time by utilizing 3 nations to help with the frontlines. Rush down to the capital and puppet them next. Finish up the focus "Estonia is Scandinavia" and with the said puppet nations, they will get annexed and cored by Estonia. And Estonia itself will be known as The Nordic League.
