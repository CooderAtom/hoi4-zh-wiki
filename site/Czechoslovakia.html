The  捷克斯洛伐克共和国 is a landlocked minor country in Central Europe with a population of 15.04 M. It borders  德国 to the west and north,  波兰 to northeast, and  奥地利,  匈牙利和 罗马尼亚 to the south. Czechoslovakia is in a precarious position, being a small democratic country surrounded by powerful potential enemies while enjoying only token protection by the erstwhile Allied Powers of France and the United Kingdom, who both prioritize maintaining peace with Germany over guaranteeing Czechoslovakian sovereignty.

历史背景

 捷克斯洛伐克 was founded in October 1918, as one of the successor states of the Austro-Hungarian Empire at the end of World War I and as part of the Treaty of St. Germain. It consisted of the present day territories of Bohemia, Moravia, Slovakia and Carpathian Ruthenia. The new country was a multi-ethnic state. The population consisted of Czechs (51%), Slovaks (16%), Germans (22%), Hungarians (5%) and Rusyns (4%). Many of the Germans, Hungarians, Poles and some Slovaks, felt oppressed because the political elite did not generally allow political autonomy for minority ethnic groups. This policy, combined with increasing Nazi propaganda especially in the industrialized German-speaking Sudetenland, led to unrest among the non-Czech population. Foreign minister Beneš became the prime architect of the Czechoslovak-Romanian-Yugoslav alliance (the "Little Entente", 1921–38) directed against Hungarian attempts to reclaim lost areas. Beneš worked closely with  法国. After 1933, Czechoslovakia remained the only democratic country in central and eastern Europe. The  德意志国 demanded Sudetenland in September 1938, which it was granted by the Munich Agreement, part of the Allies' overall policy of appeasement. Southern Slovakia and Carpathian Ruthenia were ceded to Hungary in November 1938 during the First Vienna Award. Later in March 1939, the  德意志国 established the Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia, and turned  斯洛伐克 into the Slovak Republic, a puppet state.

策略与指南

以下是one of many玩家为捷克斯洛伐克建议的战略。请记住，由于游戏的动态特性，其他玩家的情况可能会有所不同。

 捷克斯洛伐克 starts out in a peculiar position, and the one thing that will guarantee your survival is fortifying the Sudetenland. Using the Sudetenland Fortifications focuses, you will have level 7 land forts along the German border and it continues through the Sudetenland to Bratislava. The Czech Entente focuses get you some backing, and you get Yugoslavia and Romania on your side. Having the two biggest armies in the Balkans on your side is crucial to your survival as Czechoslovakia. Or, hope the French go down the Little Entente path and invite you to the Little Entente, but this is much more likely when you have Historical AI Focus off. Keep note that you don't need Historical AI focus to get Czechmate or The Munich Disagreement. Then wait around and build up your army until the Germans come in, and the Wehrmacht has to take in the fact that the Sudetenland is mountainous and going into the Sudetenland forts would be a suicide mission! While waiting for the Germans to invade, hope that France and Britain back you and build up forts in Bohemia and Moravia. If time allows, go ahead and build forts on the Polish and Hungarian borders. When the Germans make an invasion attempt of Czechoslovakia (if they do), those forts are going to help you out. With those forts backing up the Germans and your allies keeping the German invasion of Czechoslovakia weaker, go for Munich and you will get The Munich Disagreement achievement. Continue through Germany with as many divisions as you can, get the Germans to capitulate (nukes do not help with captitulation, they just kill more people) and when the treaty comes, take as much land as you can.

Acquiring the new territory will provide access to the sea, stimulate industrial growth, and expand the labor force. Furthermore, it will eliminate the risk of invasion from the west. It is now recommended that you proceed with the occupation of Hungary. At this juncture, it would be prudent to surround Hungary with your allies in order to facilitate an invasion. It is recommended that all divisions be deployed to the fronts, that allies be engaged in the conflict, and that the objective of Budapest be pursued. It is anticipated that Pecs and Debrecen will be captured along the way. In the event that Italy offers its support, it would be prudent to consider launching an invasion in that region as well. Should one wish to truly achieve a state of "Czechmate," it would be prudent to first allow global tensions to intensify (in the event that one is a democratic leader), provide justification for military action against the United Kingdom, and then commence hostilities. A joint naval invasion of England from northern France is also a viable option. Following this, the naval forces can be used to invade Egypt or, if Libya is also under control, the Suez Canal. Gibraltar can then be secured, and the Mediterranean can be dominated by capturing Malta and Cyprus. Finally, the Raj and East Africa can be invaded. However, the primary objective should be to focus the naval forces on reaching Western Australia. By establishing a foothold in the vicinity of Perth, the player can claim the achievement "Czechmate." Subsequently, the remainder of Australia should be occupied, with no concern for Canada. It is recommended that an invasion be avoided unless there is a clear intention to do so. Subsequently, it is recommended that as much of the British Empire as possible be seized, which will inevitably result in confrontation with the Soviet Union. It is, however, recommended that the attack be carried out with caution, as it may prove to be a suicide mission. It is therefore necessary to produce as much equipment as possible in order to face the British Empire.

Restoring Austria-Hungary as Czechoslovakia

Starting as Czechoslovakia in Gathering Storm, immediately create a spy agency and do the "Evaluate the Political Climate" focus, then Empower the National Fascist League. Immediately recruit the fascists demagogue to your government and discredit the government and prepare for civil war in the decisions tab. Before you start the civil war, go down the Strategic Decisions path in the focus tree and reform the Czech Entente. As soon as the "An Entente of Our Own" focus is done (It should be September 2nd, 1936), immediately start the civil war, start going down the Fortification Focus branch of your focus tree for extra manpower, army experience and for a fortified border with  德国. Make sure to start justifying war goals on  奥地利和 南斯拉夫 as soon as the civil war starts. When the Civil War ends, immediately put a front line on the Austrian border, and when the war goal is finished (October 17th, 1936), create an alliance with between  意大利 before declaring war with Austria. Italy will attempt to invite you to their war with Ethiopia, do not join that war under any circumstances. After the Czechoslovakian-Austrian War, annex all of Austria and station your troops on the border with Yugoslavia, make sure to station troops on the Yugoslav border for Italy too so they will accept an invitation into the Czechoslovakian-Yugoslav War (February 14th, 1937). After the war, take Dalmatia, Croatia, Slovenia, Ljubljana, Backa, West Banat, Herzegovina and Bosnia, if you can puppet any other Yugoslav territory, do so, make sure  科索沃 is it's own puppet so the Italians can't take it through  阿尔巴尼亚 later in the game. Try to end the war before completing the "Final Fortifications" focus so you could immediately start the "Joint Security Council" focus in the Strategic Decisions branch of your focus, which you should be able to do by bypassing the "Invite Yugoslavia" focus when you puppet Yugoslavia. After finishing the "Joint Security Council" focus, you should be able to do the "Czech Fascism" focus from the Evaluate the Political Climate branch of your focus tree. Do the "Czech Fascism" focus now, as it will help distract the Germans later. After the "Czech Fascism" focus, do the "Eliminate the Hungarian Threat" focus in the Strategic Decision branch of your focus tree, and immediately declare war on  匈牙利 when it's completed. While you're at war with Hungary, start the "Invite Romania" focus, and when the Czechoslovakian-Hungarian War ends, annex all of Hungary. When the "Invite Romania" focus is complete, continue with the "Dissolve Bulgaria" focus and immediately declare war on  保加利亚, make sure to invite  罗马尼亚 and Italy to participate in the Czechoslovakian-Bulgarian War. When Romania and Italy join the war, the Romanians should also join the Italian-Ethiopian War, when Romania does this, kick them from the faction and start justifying a war goal against them. The Allies will not come to Romania's aide or guarantee them because they're at war with Ethiopia, a member of the Allies. During or after the Czechoslovakian-Bulgarian War, use your spy agency to apply diplomatic pressure on Germany. When Germany starts justifying a war goal against you, start a nonaggression pact with them, once the Germans ask for the Sudetenland, do not give it to them. After the Czechoslovakian-Bulgarian War, try to puppet as much of Bulgaria as you can. Do the "Aggressive Wars" focus in the Evaluate the Political Climate branch of your focus tree. If Germany didn't do the "Fate of Czechoslovakia" focus in their branch before you did "The Polish Question", the Allies shouldn't have guaranteed  波兰. If Poland has not been guaranteed yet, do "The Polish Question" focus in your focus tree and declare war with Poland, Germany should join the war. After the Czechoslovakian-Polish War, try to take Krakow, Stanisławów, Lwów and Kielce, and try to puppet anything else you can get, but the German will get a majority of Poland, so don't expect too much of Poland, the war with Poland is optional for reforming Austria-Hungary. After the war with Poland, immediately put your army on the border with Romania and declare war, make sure to bring in your Bulgarian and Yugoslav puppets, along with Italy. After the Czechoslovakian-Romanian war, annex Banat, Crisana, Northern Transylvania, Transylvania and Bucovina. Make a puppet of Romania, and make two more puppets with the state of Bessarabia as  摩尔多瓦 and the state of Southern Bessarabia as  乌克兰. After this, go to you decisions tab and click "Reform Austria-Hungary", and you should have finished forming  奥匈帝国.
